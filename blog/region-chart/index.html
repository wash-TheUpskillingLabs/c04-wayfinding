      How the region fits together, on one chart

      Three lines: how The Upskilling Labs of Greater Washington is organised, the clock the Cycle runs on, and the route a person takes through both. Where they meet is where the value changes hands.

      The Wayfinding pod, Nov 7, 2026. A five-minute read.

      Three lines, one region

      It’s hard to hold the whole region in your head at once. There’s the organisation: national, the region, a steering committee, maintainers, builders, pods, projects. There’s the Cycle, which runs on dates. And there’s you, moving through both. So we drew all three as lines on one chart, the way a Metro map draws routes, and marked where they cross.

      Read it left to right. Each line says at the start what it gives and what it gets back. Where a station sits on two lines, they’re the same thing seen twice, and the rail between them carries an arrow for what passes across. Red is where people stop.

      Open the chart

      The logic, in one pass

      National gives every region the same platform and takes back what the regions prove. The region takes a copy it’s allowed to change. A steering committee decides what the region becomes; maintainers keep its changes compatible so they can travel back; builders turn frames into changes. That’s the top line, and its value flows in a loop: platform down, proof up.

      The Cycle is a clock. It gives twelve weeks, a room on group days and a vote, and it gets problems picked, finds, prototypes and three releases. Every date on it is a place where something is due, which is what makes it a clock and not a calendar.

      The person’s line is the one the other two exist for. They give four to six hours a week and what they already know. They get practice, a team and a public record of what they built. Every rail that leaves their line carries hours, a proposal or evidence upward; every rail that arrives carries a question, a frame or a team down.

      Where the value leaks

      Two stations are red. At the vote, a project the room doesn’t pick takes its pod with it: the people who formed around it are left without a team, and the chart has no rail for where they go. At after, the person leaves with a record of what they built, and nothing on the top line can read it yet. Employers haven’t been asked. There is no route back in that isn’t another Cycle.

      Those two gaps are where the value the region makes drains out. They are also, not by accident, the two places the problem situation map keeps pointing at.
