      People don’t get lost in the room. They get lost on the way to the next one.

      We’re rebuilding how The Labs gets people from their first workshop to whatever comes next.

      Find your way in

      What we believe

      Anyone should be able to see where they are, choose where to go next, and find the people going there with them.

      Right now, the routes between The Labs’ own parts get in the way. That’s what this pod is fixing.

      The first prototype

      Literal wayfinding, already live on our OLOS fork. Every library branch in the region sits on the Metro map, and the ones with a session coming up are lit and linked. Try it on the live site.

      Three ways in

      Start wherever fits your week. You can move closer any time.

      1. 1 minute

        Follow the pod

        Watch the repo and see each step as it happens: the paradox on Nov 7, the projects on Nov 14, what ships on Dec 8.

        Follow on GitHub
      2. A few hours, on your schedule

        Become a contributor

        Pick up an issue and open a pull request: log a case study, write up an interview, or improve a prototype or this site. You work in Claude Code in your browser, with nothing to install, and a maintainer reviews every pull request.

        Pick an issue
      3. 4 to 6 hours a week, to Dec 8

        Lead a project

        Be one of three builders in residence. Lead a team you recruit, ship a change The Labs runs on, and present it on Dec 8 for the national core. Join by Nov 14.

        Tell a maintainer you’re in

      Builders leave with a team, a change The Labs runs on, and a public record of how they got there. Something to talk about in an interview, not a certificate to attach.

      Not ready for a pull request? Tell us your story on Start here: the last time you didn’t know what came next at The Labs, or why you came once and didn’t come back. A maintainer replies by the next Monday.
