Wayfinding

How does an adaptive, self-directed experience stay legible to the people in it, and to the people deciding whether to back it?

What we're asking

Every cohort, problem and person is different, so the experience adapts. Participants can't feel progress in something that keeps changing, and funders can't back what they can't point to. We're finding out how to make progress visible without freezing it.

We're a Research Pod in Build Cycle C04, six weeks in which neighbors pick a hard problem about The Labs, research it properly, and build something real in response. Oct 24 to Dec 8, 2026.
