# Field map: Greater Washington (regional)

**Logged:** 2026-11-06 (desk research; one file per player in this directory)

## Field condition: the 2025–26 federal workforce reduction

- The Washington region lost about 56,000 jobs in 2025, roughly 54,000 of them (96%) from federal layoffs; the region's federal employment fell over 14% in twelve months; more than 150,000 federal workers nationally took deferred resignation and stayed on payroll through 30 September 2025, so they were absent from job counts most of the year ([Brookings, 5 Mar 2026](https://www.brookings.edu/articles/after-the-fork-greater-washington-leads-the-nation-in-regional-job-loss)).
- COG's April 2026 analysis: 62,100 federal jobs lost January 2025–January 2026 (−16.5%), about 103,900 jobs overall, the lowest federal count since 1990 ([FFXnow, 28 Apr 2026](https://www.ffxnow.com/2026/04/28/report-d-c-region-has-its-fewest-federal-workers-in-at-least-36-years/)).
- DC's unemployment rate reached 6% in July 2025 ([Technical.ly, 12 Sep 2025](https://technical.ly/civic-news/ai-upskilling-cohort-dc-workforce/)).
- Responses, by jurisdiction: DC's Gateway to District Careers hiring event (Mar 2025) and talentcapital.ai ([DOES testimony](https://dccouncil.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/DOES-2026-Performance-Oversight-Hearing-Testimony_2.26.2026vF.pdf)); Montgomery's $700,000 Mobilize Montgomery center (Oct 2025) ([WTOP](https://wtop.com/montgomery-county/2025/09/displaced-federal-workers-and-contractors-will-find-help-at-a-job-hub-in-montgomery-county)); Employ Prince George's enrolling ~500 federal workers ([Council briefing](https://cpo.soar.com/articles/6693655/Prince-Georges-County/Maryland/Employ-Prince-Georges-offers-200000-ARPA-program-and-job-services-for-displaced-federal-workers-has-served-about-500)); NOVA's $1 million NOVAnext (Mar 2025) ([CC Daily](https://www.ccdaily.com/2025/03/nova-program-to-help-dislocated-federal-workers-contractors/)); Virginia's $500,000+ Rapid Response for Northern Virginia (Nov 2025) ([Virginia Works](https://virginiaworks.gov/virginia-works-announces-over-500000-in-additional-funding-to-support-norther-virginia-job-seekers/)); COG's Talent Capital platform (Oct 2025) ([Virginia Business](https://virginiabusiness.com/d-c-area-leaders-launch-initiative-to-help-laid-off-federal-workers)); Maryland's 33 American Job Centers and Professional Outplacement Assistance Center ([WTOP, Feb 2025](https://wtop.com/local/2025/02/here-are-the-resources-available-in-each-dc-area-county-to-fired-federal-workers)).

## Patterns noticed across the field (facts, not advice)

1. **Every public response to the federal layoffs is a matching or credentialing service; none is a peer-cohort.** DOES, WorkSource Montgomery, Employ Prince George's, Virginia Career Works, NOVA and Talent Capital all describe case management, job fairs, scholarships, certifications or AI job-matching ([sources in the condition section above](#field-condition-the-202526-federal-workforce-reduction)). The only cohort-shaped response found was the 2025 DCPL–Levy AI Upskilling Cohort, 50 people from 200 applicants ([Technical.ly](https://technical.ly/civic-news/ai-upskilling-cohort-dc-workforce/)).
2. **Libraries in the region run four different frames for the same room.** DCPL: maker/creative labs gated by appointment and orientation ([DCPL Labs](https://dclibrary.org/labsatdcpl)); Arlington: open lab with only a card and an agreement ([The Shop](https://library.arlingtonva.us/for-makers-and-crafters/the-shop/)); Montgomery: 496 discrete workforce events a year counted by attendance ([MCPL FY2025](https://www.montgomerycountymd.gov/library/resources/files/about/report-to-community-2025.pdf)); Prince George's: librarians certified by the workforce board ([ULC](https://www.urbanlibraries.org/innovations/pgcmls-workforce-development)).
3. **The region's on-ramp capacity shrank before demand spiked.** Byte Back (27 years, ~7,000 served) closed July 2024 ([Technical.ly](https://technical.ly/professional-development/byte-back-closing-digital-literacy-nonprofit-dc/)); the Clark Foundation finished its spend-down in 2025 ([Clark Foundation](https://clarkfoundationdc.org/)); Employ Prince George's cut its FY2026 budget 8.3% and may close its National Harbor job center ([budget memo](https://pgccouncil.us/DocumentCenter/View/13282/FY-2026-Employ-Prince-Georges-Budget-Report)); Civic Tech DC lost its national parent in 2023 ([Technical.ly](https://technical.ly/civic-news/code-for-dc-rebrand-civic-tech-dc.md)).
4. **Published outcomes are placements and attendance; nobody publishes what happens after.** DOES reports services delivered and employment rates ([DOES testimony](https://dccouncil.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/DOES-2026-Performance-Oversight-Hearing-Testimony_2.26.2026vF.pdf)); MCPL reports attendees; Per Scholas NCR publishes no placement rate ([Per Scholas](https://perscholas.org/locations/national-capital-region/)); the Board of Trade itself complained that the system measures "short-term placements" rather than long-term outcomes ([BOT testimony](https://www.bot.org/testimony-to-dc-council-workforce-strategies-must-support-economic-growth-and-resilience/)). The only randomized evidence found in the region is Urban Alliance's (youth) and Byte Back's (closed) ([Next City](https://nextcity.org/daily/entry/evaluation-youth-internship-programs-succeed); [RAND](https://www.rand.org/pubs/commentary/2025/08/this-digital-skills-training-tripled-job-rates-why.html)).
5. **"AI" has entered course names and platform names faster than programs.** Per Scholas renamed IT support courses "AI-Enabled" and "AI-Powered" ([Per Scholas](https://perscholas.org/locations/national-capital-region/)); DOES registered an "AI Data Annotator" apprenticeship ([DOES testimony](https://dccouncil.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/DOES-2026-Performance-Oversight-Hearing-Testimony_2.26.2026vF.pdf)); Talent Capital offers "AI agents" for navigation ([Virginia Business](https://virginiabusiness.com/d-c-area-leaders-launch-initiative-to-help-laid-off-federal-workers)); the Board of Trade's April 2025 testimony did not mention AI at all ([BOT](https://www.bot.org/testimony-to-dc-council-workforce-strategies-must-support-economic-growth-and-resilience/)).
6. **The Labs is already listed as a peer.** Technical.ly's July 2026 bootcamp guide lists "Upskilling Labs" beside Per Scholas, Year Up and UDC ([Technical.ly](https://technical.ly/workforce/dc-tech-coding-bootcamp-guide-2026.md)).
