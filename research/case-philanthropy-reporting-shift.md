# Case: U.S. foundations and grantee reporting, from outcome demands to "trust-based" simplification

**Kind:** maximum variation (private funders, compared with the federal cases)
**Bears on:** post-cycle, facilitators, interfaces
**Field claims:** 2, 3, 4
**Evidence:** contradicts 4 (about half of foundation grantees said no one discussed their report with them; [CEP 2011](https://cep.org/?p=2519)); complicates 2 (a share of funders moved toward less paperwork and more unrestricted money after 2020, and grantees' reporting time fell; [Inside Philanthropy on CEP, 2023](https://insidephilanthropy.com/home/2023/8/24/a-new-report-shows-promising-shifts-in-funders-practices-whether-its-enough-is-still-up-for-debate)); supports 3 (grantees on average did not find reporting helpful, and most got no guidance on using data; [CEP via IssueLab](https://search.issuelab.org/resource/grantees-report-back-helpful-reporting-and-evaluation-processes), [Chronicle of Philanthropy 2015](https://www.philanthropy.com/news/foundations-fall-short-in-giving-grantees-advice-on-evaluations-says-report/))
**Logged:** 2026-11-05 (desk research, sources below)

## 1. What it is
Private foundations commonly require grantees to report on outcomes. Survey research by the Center for Effective Philanthropy (CEP) shows how those reports are produced and used, and the "trust-based philanthropy" movement (organized around 2020) argues for cutting paperwork in favor of conversation.

## 2. Where it came from
CEP is a nonprofit that surveys grantees about their funders (Grantee Perception Reports). Sources: CEP research blog (2011) and study description; *Chronicle of Philanthropy* on a 2015 CEP report; *Inside Philanthropy* and *Devex* on 2023 CEP reports; the Trust-Based Philanthropy Project's practice list; an HBS working paper by Alnoor Ebrahim and V. Kasturi Rangan (2010).

## 3. When
- CEP grantee data from the 2000s to 2022; trust-based practices gained momentum around 2020.
- Sources dated January 2011 to 2023 (Trust-Based Philanthropy Project page undated, current).

## 4. What it says
- CEP surveyed more than 24,000 grantees about 130 foundations and found that, on average, grantees did not find reporting and evaluation very helpful to their own work; nearly half said no discussion of their report took place. [CEP study description, IssueLab](https://search.issuelab.org/resource/grantees-report-back-helpful-reporting-and-evaluation-processes)
- A CEP analyst reported that 51% of grantees who completed a report or evaluation had discussed it with their program officer; some grantees said they heard nothing after filing a final report. [CEP, "Talk It Out," Jan 21, 2011](https://cep.org/?p=2519)
- In a 2015 CEP study of 138 nonprofits, nearly all collected performance data, but only 9% had a staff member dedicated to it, half spent under 2% of budget on measurement, and 64% got no foundation guidance on using their data, although a 2013 CEP study found about three-quarters of foundation CEOs said they gave such advice. [Chronicle of Philanthropy, Apr 2015](https://www.philanthropy.com/news/foundations-fall-short-in-giving-grantees-advice-on-evaluations-says-report/)
- The Trust-Based Philanthropy Project lists six practices, including "simplify and streamline paperwork," arguing that funder-driven reports pull nonprofits away from their work and should be replaced by dialogue. [Trust-Based Philanthropy Project, Practices](https://www.trustbasedphilanthropy.org/practices)
- Comparing 61 funders' Grantee Perception Reports before 2020 with 2021–2022, CEP found median grantee time on reporting and evaluation fell from 8 to 6 hours per funder; unrestricted support rose from 23% to 30% of grantees; 90% of foundations said in 2021 they would keep some simplified reporting. The article's author said some grantmakers "appear to be walking back" these practices, without naming them. [Inside Philanthropy, Aug 2023](https://insidephilanthropy.com/home/2023/8/24/a-new-report-shows-promising-shifts-in-funders-practices-whether-its-enough-is-still-up-for-debate)
- CEP's 2023 nonprofit survey found trust-based funding helped stabilize finances but did not resolve staff retention and pay problems. [Devex, June 21, 2023](https://www.devex.com/news/trust-based-giving-won-t-relieve-all-nonprofit-challenges-per-report-105751/amp)
- CEP frames trust and evaluation as often seen as in tension, and collects views that they can coexist. [CEP, Effective Matters, Aug 24, 2023](https://cep.org/?p=1549)
- Ebrahim and Rangan propose that whether an organization should measure long-term impact, or only outputs and short-term outcomes, depends on its type of work, rather than one standard for all. [HBS WP 10-099, 2010](https://ideas.repec.org/p/hbs/wpaper/10-099.html)

## Failure modes seen
- **Write-only reports:** about half of grantees' reports were never discussed with the funder. [CEP 2011](https://cep.org/?p=2519)
- **Measurement without capacity:** 9% of surveyed nonprofits had a dedicated data staffer. [Chronicle of Philanthropy 2015](https://www.philanthropy.com/news/foundations-fall-short-in-giving-grantees-advice-on-evaluations-says-report/)
- **Funders think they help more than grantees report:** CEOs' claims of giving data advice did not match grantees' experience. [Chronicle of Philanthropy 2015](https://www.philanthropy.com/news/foundations-fall-short-in-giving-grantees-advice-on-evaluations-says-report/)
- **Reform drift back:** commitments to simplify after 2020 were stated intentions, with anecdotal walk-backs. [Inside Philanthropy 2023](https://insidephilanthropy.com/home/2023/8/24/a-new-report-shows-promising-shifts-in-funders-practices-whether-its-enough-is-still-up-for-debate)

## 5. What it tells us about the problem
*Left for the pod. Questions to answer:*
- If roughly half of reports are never discussed, who in the field is the actual reader of a record of what happened to people?
- When a funder replaces a written report with a conversation, what record is left that a participant, or a later funder, could see?
- Who in a volunteer-run network would carry the reporting load, given that most small nonprofits have no one dedicated to it?

## 6. Hearsay and limits
- CEP data describe grantees of foundations that commission its surveys, which may not represent all funders.
- The 2011 "51%" figure is older than the trust-based shift; we found no newer verified figure on how often reports are discussed.
- We did not verify the authorship or full text of the often-cited *Stanford Social Innovation Review* "Nonprofit Starvation Cycle" (2009) and left it out.
- **Rival explanation:** the post-2020 drop in reporting time may reflect emergency COVID-era grantmaking that relaxed all requirements, not a lasting shift in what funders want to see.
- Could confirm: CEP research staff; program officers at foundations using trust-based practices; small grantees.
