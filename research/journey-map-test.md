# Testing the Participant Journey Map

How much people like a map tells us nothing about whether it works. In Maxwell Roberts' studies of transit maps, people's preference for a map didn't predict how well they used it ([Roberts et al.](https://repository.essex.ac.uk/17237/1/1-s2.0-S1071581916300751-main.pdf)). So we test this map with tasks, the way Legible London tested its signs on street corners: ask people to use it, time them, and score the answer.

## Who and How

- **Three people per round**, ideally one who has never been to The Labs, one who came to a session, and one alum.
- **Ten minutes each.** Show them the map on a laptop at the pod's site, not a printout, so they can use Where Are You?.
- **Ask them to think aloud.** Note where they look first and where they hesitate.
- **Time each task** from the end of the question to their answer, and mark it right, partly right or wrong.
- **Ask whether they liked it only at the end**, and record it separately. It's a different measure.

Privacy: no names, no recordings. Write P1, P2, P3 and a one-line description ("came to two sessions"). Nothing that identifies anyone goes in the repo.

## The Tasks

Read each one aloud, exactly as written.

| # | Task | A right answer | What it tests |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | You just came to your first workshop. Where could you go next? | Any other session on the loop, or on to Get Ready for a Cycle | Reading the loop |
| 2 | Where do most people actually start today? | At Join a Cycle, step 4 (the arc) | Arcs above the line |
| 3 | Which step doesn't exist yet? | Get Ready for a Cycle, step 3 | Dashed means planned |
| 4 | You proposed a problem and it wasn't chosen at Vote 1. What happens to it? | It ends there (the silver line stops) | Terminus bars |
| 5 | How many pods will the next Cycle have? | It isn't decided yet (the dots) | Open counts |
| 6 | Your project team is stuck. Who do you go to, in order? | Your team, then your pod, then a mentor | Reading a lane |
| 7 | Where do people most often say they got lost? | Steps 4 to 6, joining a Cycle through the project vote | Reading the quotes |
| 8 | After the showcase, name two places people go next. | Any two of the ways out | Line ends |
| 9 | Use Where Are You? to put yourself at step 2. What's next? | Get Ready for a Cycle, which is planned | The picker |
| 10 | Point to the moment in a Cycle when you'd vote on projects. | The Project Vote landmark, at step 6 | Landmarks |
| 11 | You've been to three trainings and no workshops. Is there a next session the map suggests? | A workshop: the loop runs Learn, Build, Share | The suggested way round |
| 12 | Your pod had six paradox proposals and kept three paradoxes. Were the other three cut? | No: they merged into the three in sensemaking; only a vote ends a line | Merging versus a terminus |

## Scoring

For each task, write down: seconds, right / partly / wrong, and what they said when they hesitated. A task that two of three people get wrong is a fault in the map, not in the people. Fix the map, then run the round again with three new people.

Last of all: "On a scale of one to five, how much did you like using it?" Keep it in its own column.
