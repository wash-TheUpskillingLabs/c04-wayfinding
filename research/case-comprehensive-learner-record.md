# Case: Comprehensive Learner Record (AACRAO, NASPA, 1EdTech), a fuller transcript that records learning outside courses

**Kind:** failure (slow diffusion after a decade of funded scaling)
**Bears on:** cycle, post-cycle, facilitators, interfaces
**Field claims:** 2, 3, 4
**Evidence:** supports 3; complicates 2 and 4 (employers in a pilot-campus survey said a CLR would help hiring decisions, but a 2020 review found most employers were not familiar with CLRs, and no source documents hiring use; [AACRAO, 2022](https://www.aacrao.org/news/am2022-live-inside-the-latest-research-on-comprehensive-learner-records-and-employment/), [NILOA, 2020](https://files.eric.ed.gov/fulltext/ED608790.pdf))
**Logged:** 2026-11-05 (desk research, sources below)

## 1. What it is
The Comprehensive Learner Record (CLR) is a college-issued digital record that shows learning from courses plus co-curricular activity, student employment and other experiences, mapped to learning outcomes. 1EdTech (formerly IMS Global) maintains it as an open data standard; AACRAO adopted that standard as its official one for digital learner records.

## 2. Where it came from
- AACRAO (the registrars' association) and NASPA (the student-affairs association) began the work with Lumina Foundation money in 2015.
- 1EdTech wrote the technical standard (CLR 1.0, then CLR 2.0, which 1EdTech describes as also being a Verifiable Credential). 1EdTech has also stewarded Open Badges since Mozilla transferred it in 2016.
- Sources: AACRAO press release (2015); NASPA project page; NILOA occasional paper (2020); 1EdTech standard summary; AACRAO conference write-up (2022); HR Dive (2023); Inside Higher Ed (2024); Parchment (2025).

## 3. When
- October 2015: eight implementation institutions named, $1.27 million from Lumina.
- November 2016: pilot phase ends; December 2017: phase two begins with Lumina support.
- June 2020: NILOA paper reports Lumina funding to scale to 150 institutions.
- 2022–2025: surveys on adoption.

## 4. What it says
- AACRAO and NASPA named eight institutions in October 2015 for a project to develop a more comprehensive student record, funded with $1.27 million from Lumina. ([AACRAO, 2015](https://aacrao.org/who-we-are/newsroom/2015/10/29/aacrao-and-naspa-name-comprehensive-student-record-implementation-institutions))
- NASPA's project page says the pilot ended in November 2016 with 12 institutions, and phase two (regional workshops) began December 2017. (The 8 vs 12 difference is not explained in the sources.) ([NASPA, n.d.](https://naspa.org/project/comprehensive-learner-record))
- After the first cohort, Lumina funded scaling to 150 institutions. The 2020 review reports no adoption rates or outcomes for them. ([Baker and Jankowski, NILOA, 2020](https://files.eric.ed.gov/fulltext/ED608790.pdf))
- The same review lists the problems campuses hit: mapping outcomes took longer than planned; students had to be persuaded to record co-curricular learning, harder for those working or carrying heavy loads; optional co-curricular activity is unevenly available; student information systems were built for transcripts and co-curricular data sat elsewhere; and most employers were not yet familiar with CLRs. ([NILOA, 2020](https://files.eric.ed.gov/fulltext/ED608790.pdf))
- Elon University, a pilot campus, surveyed employers in 2016 and 2017; employers said a CLR would likely help new-hire decisions. The write-up gives no sample sizes or percentages. ([AACRAO, 2022](https://www.aacrao.org/news/am2022-live-inside-the-latest-research-on-comprehensive-learner-records-and-employment/))
- An AACRAO survey of 319 education professionals found 58% had implemented or were considering some form of digital credentialing (including CLR). The article notes that student information and ERP systems generally had not been changed to support CLRs. ([HR Dive, January 2023](https://www.hrdive.com/news/comprehensive-learner-record-clr-recruiting/639485/))
- A 2023 survey of 144 institutions found 22% had a campus-wide competency framework; the study's author said CLRs are hard to use without one, and the article notes the term itself is not widely understood. ([Inside Higher Ed, January 2024](https://www.insidehighered.com/node/137891))
- A 2025 Parchment survey reports "just over 1 in 10 institutions" plan to implement CLRs, and that transcripts remain the dominant credential. ([Parchment, September 2025](https://www.parchment.com/blog/how-higher-ed-is-shaping-skills-communication-survey))

## Failure modes seen
- **Plumbing built for the old record:** Student information systems were designed for transcripts, so co-curricular data lived in separate systems ([NILOA, 2020](https://files.eric.ed.gov/fulltext/ED608790.pdf); [HR Dive, 2023](https://www.hrdive.com/news/comprehensive-learner-record-clr-recruiting/639485/)).
- **Framework prerequisite:** CLRs need a competency framework, and only 22% of surveyed institutions had one campus-wide ([Inside Higher Ed, 2024](https://www.insidehighered.com/node/137891)).
- **Recording burden falls on the learner:** Students had to see value in logging co-curricular learning, which was hardest for those with jobs or heavy loads ([NILOA, 2020](https://files.eric.ed.gov/fulltext/ED608790.pdf)).
- **Reader not in the room:** Most employers were not familiar with CLRs ([NILOA, 2020](https://files.eric.ed.gov/fulltext/ED608790.pdf)).
- **Stated intent, no follow-through data:** Employers said CLRs would help, but no source reports an employer hiring differently because of one ([AACRAO, 2022](https://www.aacrao.org/news/am2022-live-inside-the-latest-research-on-comprehensive-learner-records-and-employment/)).
- **Funded scaling, low uptake:** Lumina funded scaling to 150 institutions by 2020, yet in 2025 just over 1 in 10 institutions report plans to implement ([NILOA, 2020](https://files.eric.ed.gov/fulltext/ED608790.pdf); [Parchment, 2025](https://www.parchment.com/blog/how-higher-ed-is-shaping-skills-communication-survey)).

## 5. What it tells us about the problem
*Left for the pod. Questions to answer:*
- Who in The Labs would do the registrar's job (mapping outcomes, validating evidence), and is that a role people would volunteer for?
- When learning happens in many places (workshops, pods, maintained projects), what happens to the parts nobody records?
- Does the record mainly serve the learner's own sense of standing, or an outside reader, and do those need different records?

## 6. Hearsay and limits
- No source gives a count of institutions issuing CLRs in production today, or of students who have received one. The surveys differ in sample and wording, so the 58% (2022) and "just over 1 in 10" (2025) figures are not directly comparable.
- The 8 vs 12 pilot institution count conflicts between AACRAO and NASPA sources.
- **Rival explanation:** Uptake may be limited by registrar-office capacity and student-information-system vendor timelines (a procurement and staffing problem), not by lack of demand from employers or students.
- Could confirm: AACRAO, NASPA, 1EdTech (certified product lists), NILOA, Parchment, and pilot campuses such as Elon University and IUPUI.
