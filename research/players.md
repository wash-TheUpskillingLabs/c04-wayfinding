## What the field maps found (facts, from the two summaries)

- **Regional condition:** about 62,100 federal jobs lost Jan 2025–Jan 2026 (COG); nearly all 2025 regional job losses were federal (Brookings).
- **Every public layoff response in the region is matching or credentialing, not cohorts.** The Labs is already listed as a peer in Technical.ly's July 2026 guide.
- **On-ramp capacity shrank before demand spiked:** Byte Back closed (2024); the Clark Foundation spent down to zero (2025); Employ Prince George's budget cut 8%; the Digital Equity Act and IMLS grants cut in 2025 (IMLS reinstated by injunction in Dec 2025).
- **Four library systems frame the same room four ways** (digital equity, workforce, makerspace, "Labs").
- **The field's unit of seeing is placements, attendance, credentials and wages,** regionally and nationally.
- **New AI-labelled money is arriving as hubs and credentials:** NSF's AI-Ready America hubs (up to 56 at $1M/yr, told to inventory and coordinate rather than deliver training); Microsoft–PLA's library AI-skills program; America Achieves' Good Jobs Economy with Maryland as a partner state.
- **National volunteer civic-tech infrastructure collapsed and was not rebuilt** (Code for America's brigades; the Alliance of Civic Technologists has raised about $7,000 total).

## Weak links and gaps (for the pod)

- No context player has been interviewed. Every "what they want" in the top table is blank or secondhand.
- Missing field players the research could not source: General Assembly DC, DC Tech meetup, Capital One, Alexandria and Loudoun libraries, Computer CORE, Greater Washington Together; nationally Schmidt Sciences, Omidyar, Ford, Mozilla.
- Several regional files are thin (Fairfax, Federal City Council, Meyer, Cafritz); their "what they want" is from a website, not a person.
- The field maps describe how the field sees, which is the test for the pod's "seeing" theme: does the field's way of seeing (placements, attendance) match what people at The Labs need to see (the route, readiness, who's with them)?
