# Note: the mentor network as a wayfinding problem

*Nov 6, 2026. The problem owner names the mentor network, bench, request escalation, triage and matchmaking as the area The Labs most underinvested in over its first year. Shared with Scaffolding, Front Door and the Mentor Program's design group, which owns the program itself.*

**Getting stuck is a threshold.** The route question: when you're stuck, can you see who can help, how to ask, and what happens next?

## What the library already says
- **The Labs' own account:** mentors are ad hoc and just in time; an upskiller gets stuck before being connected to one ("The Upskilling Labs, Explained"). Alumni: a team asked Labs experts for help and it took a long time because the problem was already complicated; moderators should step in faster (`record-alumni-survey-2026.md`).
- **Assigned early, mentors work:** Master Gardener trainee dropout fell from 17–27% to 2% the year mentors were assigned (`case-extension-4h-volunteers.md`). Mentored Stack Overflow questions scored 50% higher (`case-stack-overflow-newcomers.md`).
- **Single pairs break:** Recurse Center's one-to-one beginner pairs fell apart (`case-recurse-center.md`).
- **Help can solve the task and lose the person:** expert help raised "good first issue" completion and correlated with lower retention (`site-evidence.md`).
- **Mentors overwhelm at the gate:** Outreachy (`case-outreachy-contribution-period.md`).
- **Nudges need a known person behind them:** summer melt (`case-summer-melt.md`).
- **The bench thins quietly:** a third of trained Carpentries instructors never taught; Kubernetes pruned hundreds of inactive members three times.

## Split
- **Wayfinding:** the route to help. Seeing who can help with what, how a request is routed and escalated, the match, and knowing your request landed and when you'll hear back.
- **Scaffolding:** what a good mentoring session looks like, mentor training and support, not doing the work for people.
- **Front Door:** where the bench, requests and record of who helped live in OLOS.
- **Mentor Program design group:** the program itself. The pods' work should feed theirs.

## Not yet researched
Matching in volunteer mentoring networks; tiered triage and escalation (support desks, open-source help channels, nurse lines); keeping a volunteer bench current; office hours and group help versus one-to-one matching. First interview: the Mentor Program's lead.
