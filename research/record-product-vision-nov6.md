# Record: the problem owner's vision for OLOS (Nov 6)

**Bears on:** pre-cycle, cycle, post-cycle, facilitators, activation-mobilization, interfaces
**Logged:** 2026-11-06. Dorst's steps 7–8 (futures, transformation), stated early by the problem owner. It is the destination, not the route: the pods' frames decide how each piece gets there, and some lines will change once real people try the prototypes.

## What OLOS is for
OLOS is how a community learns together, builds together, and keeps what it learns.

You can start anywhere: a Slack thread, a GitHub issue, a calendar invite, a link a friend sent, an agent at midnight. Wherever you start, OLOS should help you see where you are, choose where to go next, and find the people going there with you. Then it brings you into the room with them: your pod, your team, your branch, the whole community gathered.

One person learns. A community keeps it. Every community can use it.

## What it holds
- **Kits.** Workshops, trainings, project nights and the Cycle itself, ready for any lab to run: the plan, the materials, the role of the person running it, and what comes after.
- **The Cycle.** Twelve weeks to take a problem from research to something real, end to end. An incubation sprint and a capstone, not the front door.
- **Member projects.** Ventures and civic projects that came out of Cycles or come from outside. The Labs doesn't run them; it helps them bring in contributors and turns their real work into a place to learn. Slower-burn programs form around them.
- **Routes.** The ways between all of it: workshop to member project, project into a Cycle, Cycle into running things, into a workplace and back.
- **The mentor network.** A bench of mentors and instructors, a way to ask for help, triage and escalation, and matching.
- **The record.** What each person has done and learned, what each project needs, what each lab has run. Visible to the people in it; counted for the field only above a privacy floor.

## How it should feel to use
- **You always know where you are.** Position, what's next, and who else is on the route, from any tool.
- **The first thing you do is small and real.**
- **Help sits beside the work.**
- **A person is behind every threshold,** especially the one from screen to room.
- **Online is the way in. In person is where it holds.** The record carries what happens in the room back to everyone who couldn't be there.
- **OLOS meets you in the tools you already use** (Slack, GitHub, Google Workspace, agents). It is the record underneath, so nothing important lives only in one tool. The OLOS app is where the whole picture comes together; it is not the only place you can act.
- **Stepping back is honorable.**
- **What moves, moves because something changed.**
