# Player: Institute of Museum and Library Services, IMLS (agency)

**Circle:** field
**Scope:** national
**Role in the problem:** funder
**Logged:** 2026-11-06

*See also the existing case `field-funders/case-imls-library-counts.md`.*

## Who they are, and where people meet them
The federal agency that funds libraries, mostly through formula grants to state library agencies (which fund DC Public Library programs) and competitive national grants. Its status in 2025–26: targeted for elimination by executive order in March 2025, staff reduced to roughly half of 70+, grants terminated, then a permanent injunction (Nov 21, 2025, *State of Rhode Island v. Trump*, 21 states) and reinstatement of all terminated grants announced Dec 3, 2025 ([Publishers Weekly, Dec 8 2025](https://www.publishersweekly.com/pw/print/20251208/99218-imls-restores-competitive-grant-funding.html); [NISO, Jan 2026](https://www.niso.org/niso-io/2026/01/imls-grants-reinstated-after-federal-judges-ruling)).

## What they bring
- Financial: the only federal money line for libraries; FY2026 appropriation was still unapproved by Congress as of the NISO item, and the FY2026 budget proposal sought to shutter the agency ([NISO](https://www.niso.org/niso-io/2026/01/imls-grants-reinstated-after-federal-judges-ruling); [Book Riot](https://bookriot.com/the-2026-federal-budget-proposal-shutters-the-institute-for-museum-and-library-services)).
- A separate suit, *ALA v. Sonderling* (ALA and AFSCME, DDC), was still pending ([Publishers Weekly](https://www.publishersweekly.com/pw/print/20251208/99218-imls-restores-competitive-grant-funding.html)).

## What they want
Not available as a current strategy statement from the sources opened; the agency communicated the reinstatement via website and grantee emails, offering extensions to March 31 or one year and adjusted reporting ([Publishers Weekly](https://www.publishersweekly.com/pw/print/20251208/99218-imls-restores-competitive-grant-funding.html)).

## What they've tried about this problem, and how it went
- Its grant lines (e.g. Laura Bush 21st Century Librarian, National Leadership Grants) historically funded library workforce and digital-literacy pilots; in 2025 those lines stopped and restarted within nine months. The Massachusetts Board of Library Commissioners tracked the cuts state by state ([MBLC federal cuts page](https://mblc.state.ma.us/federal-cuts.php), not opened).
- Program outcomes were not the issue in 2025; continuity was.

## How they see the problem
Inference: in 2025–26 the agency's frame is survival and compliance with court orders, not program design.

## Open questions for the pod

- Will IMLS competitive NOFOs return in 2026, and on what priorities?
