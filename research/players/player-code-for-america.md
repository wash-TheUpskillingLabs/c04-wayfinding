# Player: Code for America, post-Brigade (institution)

**Circle:** field
**Scope:** national
**Role in the problem:** peer program (formerly), partner project, competitor for civic-tech volunteers' attention
**Logged:** 2026-11-06

*See the existing case `cases-civic/case-cfa-brigade-network.md` for the Brigade Network's end.*

## Who they are, and where people meet them
A national civic-tech nonprofit that now works directly with governments on safety-net, tax and criminal-justice services, and runs an AI practice. In 2025 it worked in 27 states and DC, reports 7 million people helped and $22 billion in benefits delivered, and built an error-prevention tool for DC's SNAP caseworkers ([2025 Impact Report](https://codeforamerica.org/impact/2025/)). A Labs person meets it through its Summit (800+ attendees in 2025), FormFest (2,000+ registrants) or as a DC government vendor, not as a volunteer network.

## What they bring
- Financial: a $100M donation in 2022 for its Safety Net Innovation Lab ([StateScoop](https://statescoop.com/code-for-america-former-brigades-regroup/)); current budget not stated in the impact report.
- Social: 500+ partners across all 50 states over 15+ years; a Safety Net Innovation Lab cohort of government leaders that meets monthly ([2025 Impact Report](https://codeforamerica.org/impact/2025/)).
- What it no longer brings: the Brigade program, which in 2022 had a budget over $3M, 14 full-time staff, fiscal sponsorship for about 60 chapters, online organizing spaces and an annual conference ([StateScoop, May 17 2023](https://statescoop.com/code-for-america-former-brigades-regroup/)).

## What they want
"human-centered technology to improve public services and make government work well for everyone" (mission, [2025 Impact Report](https://codeforamerica.org/impact/2025/)).

## What they've tried about this problem, and how it went
- Ran the largest US civic-tech volunteer network for a decade, then ended affiliation in February 2023, citing lower post-pandemic volunteer engagement, privacy and security concerns about volunteers working with governments, and a shift to direct government work ([StateScoop](https://statescoop.com/code-for-america-former-brigades-regroup/)).
- 2025 AI work: a 50-state Government AI Landscape Assessment, an AI Studio Accessibility Project piloted in Salt Lake City and Georgia ([2025 Impact Report](https://codeforamerica.org/impact/2025/)). Volunteers, brigades and a network do not appear in the 2025 report.

## How they see the problem
Frame (inference from the report): impact is measured in people served and dollars delivered through government partners; volunteer onboarding and pathways are no longer its problem.

## Open questions for the pod
- What happened to the DC brigade (Code for DC) members after 2023, and where did they go?
- Would Code for America's DC government relationships see a library-based volunteer network as a partner or a liability (its stated 2023 concern)?
