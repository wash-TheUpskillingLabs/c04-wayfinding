# Player: Employ Prince George's (agency)

**Circle:** field
**Scope:** regional
**Role in the problem:** referrer | funder | partner project
**Logged:** 2026-11-06

## Who they are, and where people meet them
A 501(c)(3) that is Prince George's County's principal workforce entity: staff, fiscal agent and administrative agent for the county Workforce Development Board, which manages WIOA Title I funds, and operator of the public workforce system ([County Council budget memo, 28 Apr 2025](https://pgccouncil.us/DocumentCenter/View/13282/FY-2026-Employ-Prince-Georges-Budget-Report)). It also certifies library staff as career development professionals ([ULC, 2020](https://www.urbanlibraries.org/innovations/pgcmls-workforce-development)). A Labs participant would meet them at a job fair or through a library referral.

## What they bring
- **Financial:** FY2026 proposed budget $16.8 million, down 8.3%; county contribution $3.27 million (19.5%); 20 layoffs proposed ([budget memo](https://pgccouncil.us/DocumentCenter/View/13282/FY-2026-Employ-Prince-Georges-Budget-Report)).
- **Human:** 22 county-funded FTE plus 95 grant-funded FTE ([budget memo](https://pgccouncil.us/DocumentCenter/View/13282/FY-2026-Employ-Prince-Georges-Budget-Report)).
- **Social:** a January job fair drew about 1,000 job seekers and 60 employers ([Council briefing, 27 Oct 2025](https://cpo.soar.com/articles/6693655/Prince-Georges-County/Maryland/Employ-Prince-Georges-offers-200000-ARPA-program-and-job-services-for-displaced-federal-workers-has-served-about-500)).

## What they want
The memo shows a focus on replacing lost revenue (a $100,000 WIOA Adult cut; new state, county and bank grants) and finishing a Virtual Career Center, 75% complete ([budget memo](https://pgccouncil.us/DocumentCenter/View/13282/FY-2026-Employ-Prince-Georges-Budget-Report)).

## What they've tried about this problem, and how it went
- **Federal workers:** about 500 displaced federal workers enrolled; outreach to ~2,000 registered with state unemployment; ~$200,000 remaining ARPA money for paid placements for roughly 50 ([Council briefing](https://cpo.soar.com/articles/6693655/Prince-Georges-County/Maryland/Employ-Prince-Georges-offers-200000-ARPA-program-and-job-services-for-displaced-federal-workers-has-served-about-500)).
- **Contraction:** the American Job Center at National Harbor has halted most programming and may close; Youth Career Connections (72 schools) ends in FY2026 after a state bill failed, with ~60 layoffs ([budget memo](https://pgccouncil.us/DocumentCenter/View/13282/FY-2026-Employ-Prince-Georges-Budget-Report)).
- **Library partnership:** staff certification for PGCMLS (ULC, 2020).

## How they see the problem
Services (counseling, résumé conversion from federal format, stipended training) delivered through a job-center network, with capacity limited by grant revenue (memo, briefing). Inference: a scarcity frame; the ask to council members was for employer postings and referrals.

## Open questions for the pod
- With 500 federal workers enrolled and ~50 paid slots, what happens to the other 450?
- Is the library certification partnership still active after the FY2026 cuts?
