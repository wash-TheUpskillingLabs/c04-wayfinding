# Player: Merit America (institution)

**Circle:** field
**Scope:** national (online)
**Role in the problem:** peer program, competitor (for learners)
**Logged:** 2026-11-06

## Who they are, and where people meet them
A DC-headquartered (per job listings) online upskilling nonprofit for working adults; more than 20,000 learners since 2018 and an estimated $1.4B in cumulative wage gains; nine career tracks, three new in 2025 (HR, Semiconductor & Advanced Manufacturing, Supply Chain Planning); building an online college with stackable 16-week credentials toward an AAS and an "AI-forward curriculum" ([2025 Impact Report](https://meritamerica.org/2025-impact-report/)). People meet it online, through employer partners, or through its ranking as number one in education on Fast Company's 2026 Most Innovative Companies list ([blog](https://meritamerica.org/blog/merit-america-ranked-1-in-education-on-fast-companys-annual-list-of-the-worlds-most-innovative-companies-of-2026/), not opened).

## What they bring
- Human/social: coaching plus (in earlier descriptions) peer squads; the impact page names coaching and flexible pacing ([2025 Impact Report](https://meritamerica.org/2025-impact-report/)).
- Financial: strategic funding and hiring partners listed together: Google, Amazon, Tesla, Lyft, J.P. Morgan Chase, Infosys, Guild, Instacart, Accenture, Social Finance ([2025 Impact Report](https://meritamerica.org/2025-impact-report/)). Budget not stated.

## What they want
Wage gains for working adults without degrees; the headline metric is cumulative wage gain ([2025 Impact Report](https://meritamerica.org/2025-impact-report/)).

## What they've tried about this problem, and how it went
- A hybrid Semiconductor track with Austin Community College: 74% of alumni reported successful outcomes within three months ([2025 Impact Report](https://meritamerica.org/2025-impact-report/)).
- Early College cohorts start with zero tuition, philanthropically funded until accreditation and federal aid eligibility ([2025 Impact Report](https://meritamerica.org/2025-impact-report/)).
- No alumni network or post-program structure is described on the page; a "Merit America Guarantee" page is linked but not opened.

## How they see the problem
Frame: the working adult needs flexibility, a coach and a credential that stacks; the route ends in a job change. Inference: it is moving toward the credential frame (Lumina/Gates) by becoming a college.

## Open questions for the pod
- How many DC-region learners does it serve, and does it refer anyone to in-person options?
- What happens to a learner who finishes but does not change jobs?
