# Player: America Achieves (institution)

**Circle:** field
**Scope:** national
**Role in the problem:** funder (co-funder/intermediary), policy, partner project
**Logged:** 2026-11-06

## Who they are, and where people meet them
A national nonprofit led by Jon Schnur that works with governors, states and regional coalitions on "good jobs" talent systems; it co-funds programs, shapes bipartisan policy and publishes what works ([americaachieves.org](https://americaachieves.org/)). Its current flagship is the Good Jobs Economy initiative, launched with the National Governors Association as part of the NGA Chair's Initiative on July 30, 2025, with Oklahoma and Maryland as the first two partner states ([NGA press release](https://www.nga.org/news/press-releases/bipartisan-governors-unite-to-reignite-the-american-dream-and-expand-access-to-good-jobs/)). A person at The Labs would most likely meet it indirectly: through Maryland's Good Jobs partnership (EARN Maryland, the RAISE Act), through a regional coalition it advises, or through its Workforce Pell guidance for governors and philanthropies ([ASU+GSV update, Apr 14 2026](https://www.americaachieves.org/articles/at-asu-gsv-america-achieves-demonstrates-early-progress-on-good-jobs-economy-core-commitments)).

## What they bring
- Financial: FY ending Aug 2025 expenses of about $11.06M on revenue of $0.79M, 19 employees, assets $0.91M (Form 990 via [CauseIQ](https://causeiq.com/organizations/america-achieves,273238471)). It says it has helped design over $2 billion in bipartisan programs and supported about $250M in funding for local partners ([ASU+GSV update](https://www.americaachieves.org/articles/at-asu-gsv-america-achieves-demonstrates-early-progress-on-good-jobs-economy-core-commitments)).
- Political: convening power with governors via NGA; it was a key partner on Oklahoma's successful $6M DOL Industry-Driven Skills Training Fund application (Dec 9, 2025) and now serves as "strategic advisor" to that program ([press release](https://www.americaachieves.org/articles/america-achieves-helps-oklahoma-secure-6-million-to-boost-workforce-training)).
- Social: program areas include tech-hubs technical assistance (6 of 13 supported regions won implementation grants) and a Brookings Metro peer-learning cohort for 11 regions implementing Build Back Better Regional Challenge and Good Jobs Challenge grants ([CauseIQ program areas](https://causeiq.com/organizations/america-achieves,273238471)).
- Funders named publicly: Blue Meridian Partners, MacKenzie Scott (broader work); John M. Belk Endowment, Leon Levine Foundation, Best Buy Foundation (Good Jobs WNC); later The Rockefeller Foundation and North Carolina GSK Foundation, $3.3M in commitments Sept 24, 2026 ([briefglance summary](https://briefglance.com/articles/america-achieves-unveils-plan-to-reshape-us-workforce-development); [news index](https://americaachieves.org/latest-news)).

## What they want
Its stated vision is that everyone has a clear path to a good job and a career with upward mobility ([homepage](https://americaachieves.org/)). Core Good Jobs Economy commitments: help states build Good Jobs initiatives and funds; "modernize talent systems" so employers and workers connect more directly; clear pathways regardless of location, background or degree; align workforce, education and economic development around measurable regional results; shape Workforce Pell implementation ([ASU+GSV update](https://www.americaachieves.org/articles/at-asu-gsv-america-achieves-demonstrates-early-progress-on-good-jobs-economy-core-commitments)).

## What they've tried about this problem, and how it went
- Good Jobs Western North Carolina: 23 counties, all 11 regional community colleges, goal of 1,200 residents into good jobs over three years; $2.5M secured toward a $6M in-state goal to be matched by $6M national ([ASU+GSV update](https://www.americaachieves.org/articles/at-asu-gsv-america-achieves-demonstrates-early-progress-on-good-jobs-economy-core-commitments)). Outcomes not yet published.
- Maryland partnership: $25M public (EARN, RAISE Act) plus $15M philanthropic, expected to support 14,000+ Marylanders in cybersecurity, healthcare, IT, life sciences and aerospace ([NGA](https://www.nga.org/news/press-releases/bipartisan-governors-unite-to-reignite-the-american-dream-and-expand-access-to-good-jobs/)).
- Workforce Pell guidance with NGA: urges states to prioritize high-wage jobs, track placement and wage gains, and require employer validation ([briefglance](https://briefglance.com/articles/america-achieves-unveils-plan-to-reshape-us-workforce-development)).
- AI: the only explicit AI item found is its ASU+GSV panel, "A New American Talent System for an AI-Disrupted World"; the Oklahoma grant targets "AI infrastructure" jobs. No AI-literacy or community-based AI program was found.

## How they see the problem
The published frame is state- and employer-led: the unit of change is the state "talent system," the delivery partner is the community college and the employer, and success is placement and wage data. Inference: volunteer or library-based networks do not appear in its materials; a regional network like The Labs would be visible to it only as a local provider inside a state's Good Jobs Fund pipeline.

## Open questions for the pod

- What does America Achieves count as evidence that a "pathway" is clear, and is it only placement and wages?
- Is there an AI-literacy component planned, or is "AI" only an infrastructure-jobs sector label?
