# Player: Per Scholas National Capital Region (program)

**Circle:** field
**Scope:** national (regional campus)
**Role in the problem:** peer program | competitor | referrer
**Logged:** 2026-11-06

## Who they are, and where people meet them
The Silver Spring, MD campus (1400 Spring St, Suite 501) of a national no-cost IT training nonprofit with 35,000+ learners served and 25+ campuses; NCR courses listed are "AI-Enabled IT Support" (next start 7 Dec 2026) and "NextGen IT Support (AI-Powered)" (Feb 2027), 12–16 weeks full-time, remote or in person ([Per Scholas NCR](https://perscholas.org/locations/national-capital-region/)). A Labs participant seeking a credentialed route would meet them as an applicant; Technical.ly's 2026 bootcamp guide lists both Per Scholas and "Upskilling Labs" side by side ([Technical.ly, 1 Jul 2026](https://technical.ly/workforce/dc-tech-coding-bootcamp-guide-2026.md)).

## What they bring
- **Financial:** up to $15k of no-cost training per learner plus two years of coaching, grant-funded; no income share ([Per Scholas NCR](https://perscholas.org/locations/national-capital-region/)).
- **Social:** an NCR advisory board with IBM, Microsoft, Accenture Federal Services and others; hiring-partner logos for Amazon, Exelon, TEKsystems ([Per Scholas NCR](https://perscholas.org/locations/national-capital-region/)).

## What they want
The page says employers who hire graduates see improved retention and faster productivity; it does not publish NCR placement, salary or completion rates ([Per Scholas NCR](https://perscholas.org/locations/national-capital-region/)).

## What they've tried about this problem, and how it went
- **Full-time cohort with two-year alumni support:** the post-program route is formal (coaching, professional development for 2 years) ([Per Scholas NCR](https://perscholas.org/locations/national-capital-region/)).
- **AI rebadging of IT support curriculum** in 2026 course names ([Per Scholas NCR](https://perscholas.org/locations/national-capital-region/)).
- Eligibility text is inconsistent on the page (open to all vs income requirements), which is itself an on-ramp ambiguity ([Per Scholas NCR](https://perscholas.org/locations/national-capital-region/)).

## How they see the problem
A 40-hour-a-week bootcamp into entry-level IT jobs, with employers as the customer (site). Inference: full-time intensity excludes people who are working; The Labs' weekly workshops occupy the opposite end.
