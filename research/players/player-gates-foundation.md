# Player: Gates Foundation, US Program (funder)

**Circle:** field
**Scope:** national
**Role in the problem:** funder
**Logged:** 2026-11-06

## Who they are, and where people meet them
The largest US private foundation, now on a 20-year countdown to dissolution in 2045 with about $200 billion to spend. Its July 29, 2026 US education strategy sets 2030 goals (Algebra 1 by ninth grade, more first-generation college-goers, better freshman pass rates, 25% more transferred credits) and a long-run aim of 10 million people earning credentials and doubling the growth rate of 18–34-year-olds with a "credential of value" ([The 74](https://www.the74million.org/article/gates-foundation-bets-on-credentials-ai-to-reach-students-before-2045-sunset)). Its US Program also runs an Economic Mobility and Opportunity strategy ([Gates US Program](https://usprogram.gatesfoundation.org/)). People meet it through colleges, intermediaries and tools it funds, not directly.

## What they bring
- Financial: scale ($1.1B math investment in 2022 as a reference point) ([The 74](https://www.the74million.org/article/gates-foundation-bets-on-credentials-ai-to-reach-students-before-2045-sunset)).
- Cultural: AI framed as teacher- and advisor-facing tools (dashboards, FAFSA-tracking agents, tutoring), with a stated goal of guidance and quality control for school AI use; survey figures cited: teacher AI use rose from about one-third (spring 2024) to 60%, 68% had no AI training ([The 74](https://www.the74million.org/article/gates-foundation-bets-on-credentials-ai-to-reach-students-before-2045-sunset)).

## What they want
Credentials of value for millions more learners before 2045, with AI used to free advisors (some handle 400+ students) for personal support ([The 74](https://www.the74million.org/article/gates-foundation-bets-on-credentials-ai-to-reach-students-before-2045-sunset)).

## What they've tried about this problem, and how it went
- Pilots like the Atlas math dashboard (about 180 NYC teachers) ([The 74](https://www.the74million.org/article/gates-foundation-bets-on-credentials-ai-to-reach-students-before-2045-sunset)). Community organizations are not named as delivery partners in the strategy coverage.
- Co-funds NextLadder Ventures with Ballmer Group, Stand Together, Valhalla Foundation and John Overdeck, on AI for economic mobility ([SHRM Foundation listing](https://www.shrm.org/foundation/skills-first-future/resource-library/resource-detail-page.89287__ag), not opened; unverified details).

## How they see the problem
Frame: institutions (schools, colleges) and the adults inside them; AI as a tool for the advisor, not the learner's self-navigation. Inference: a community network is outside its delivery model.
