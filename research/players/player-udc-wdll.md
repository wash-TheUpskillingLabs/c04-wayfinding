# Player: UDC Workforce Development and Lifelong Learning (institution)

**Circle:** field
**Scope:** regional
**Role in the problem:** peer program | referrer | competitor
**Logged:** 2026-11-06

## Who they are, and where people meet them
The University of the District of Columbia's workforce division, offering mostly free courses to adult DC residents (18+) in four pathways: Construction and Property Management, Early Childhood Education, Healthcare, and Information Technology ([UDC WDLL](https://www.udc.edu/wdll/)). Technical.ly's 2026 guide lists UDC as "tuition-free training for DC residents" alongside Per Scholas, Year Up and "Upskilling Labs" ([Technical.ly, 1 Jul 2026](https://technical.ly/workforce/dc-tech-coding-bootcamp-guide-2026.md)). A DC-resident Labs participant could be enrolled at both.

## What they bring
- **Financial:** free for DC residents, "a handful" of classes excepted; a recent Amerigroup DC workforce grant ([UDC WDLL](https://www.udc.edu/wdll/)).
- **Built:** campus at 4200 Connecticut Ave NW and other WDLL sites (locations page not fetched).
- Enrollment numbers: not on the page — **unverified**.

## What they want
Reduce unemployment and underemployment, build professional skills, prepare people for jobs, promotions and new industries ([UDC WDLL](https://www.udc.edu/wdll/)).

## What they've tried about this problem, and how it went
- **Documented intake gate:** applicants submit documentation, health-care documentation and a CASAS assessment ([UDC WDLL](https://www.udc.edu/wdll/)).
- **"No Wrong Door" policy:** a UDC page title references a WDLL "No Wrong Door" policy ([UDC](https://www.udc.edu/?p=28794), search result; not fetched — content **unverified**).
- AI courses: not named on the WDLL page.

## How they see the problem
Career pathways in in-demand sectors for residents, with eligibility and assessment at entry (site). Inference: an institutional, credential-bearing on-ramp with higher friction than a library workshop.

## Open questions for the pod
- What does the "No Wrong Door" policy actually route, and to whom?
- Would UDC accept a Labs Build Cycle as prior learning or a bridge into its IT pathway?
