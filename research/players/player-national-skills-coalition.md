# Player: National Skills Coalition (network)

**Circle:** field
**Scope:** national
**Role in the problem:** policy, standard-setter
**Logged:** 2026-11-06

## Who they are, and where people meet them
A 25-year-old advocacy coalition for workforce and skills policy, with state coalitions (SkillSPAN, 20 states), a business network (Business Leaders United) and a worker-voice network (Voices for Skills) ([interim CEO reflections, May 2025](https://nationalskillscoalition.org/blog/news/a-coalition-built-for-this-moment-reflections-from-nscs-interim-ceo-brooke-derenzis/)). People meet it through its reports (used by libraries, Fed banks and funders), its annual Skills Summit and Hill day, and through state coalition partners.

## What they bring
- Political: 350+ members from 30 states held 150+ congressional meetings at the 2025 Skills Summit ([same](https://nationalskillscoalition.org/blog/news/a-coalition-built-for-this-moment-reflections-from-nscs-interim-ceo-brooke-derenzis/)).
- Human/cultural: the most-cited digital-skills baseline in the field: 92% of analyzed job postings (43M postings, 2021, Lightcast data) require digital skills; roughly one-third of workers lack foundational digital skills; jobs needing one digital skill pay about 23% more ([press release, Feb 6 2023](https://nationalskillscoalition.org/news/press-releases/new-report-92-of-jobs-require-digital-skills-one-third-of-workers-have-low-or-no-digital-skills-due-to-historic-underinvestment-structural-inequities/)). Co-produced with the Atlanta Fed's Center for Workforce and Economic Opportunity.
- Budget not found in sources opened (unverified).

## What they want
Its AI page says AI should strengthen working people rather than sideline them, via digital and AI skills access, high-quality training, closing the digital divide, worker voice and "broadly shared economic opportunity" ([AI issue page](https://nationalskillscoalition.org/issue/articificial-intelligence/)). 2025 priorities: protect federal workforce funding, Short-Term Pell, apprenticeship and skills-based hiring ([May 2025](https://nationalskillscoalition.org/blog/news/a-coalition-built-for-this-moment-reflections-from-nscs-interim-ceo-brooke-derenzis/)).

## What they've tried about this problem, and how it went
- Published a line of digital-literacy reports (industry-by-industry, racial equity lens, state policy) that frame digital skills as a baseline need alongside access ([AI issue page](https://nationalskillscoalition.org/issue/articificial-intelligence/)).
- Its "Closing the Digital Skill Divide" findings were picked up by PLA's Digital Literacy Workshop cohort and by Comcast, which funds its digital-equity work ([ALA Connect thread](https://connect.ala.org/pla/discussion/fw-new-report-92-of-jobs-require-digital-skills); [press release](https://nationalskillscoalition.org/news/press-releases/new-report-92-of-jobs-require-digital-skills-one-third-of-workers-have-low-or-no-digital-skills-due-to-historic-underinvestment-structural-inequities/)).
- No program delivery; no on-ramp or alumni experiments found.

## How they see the problem
Frame: a public-investment and policy problem, measured in postings and wages. Who delivers training is left open ("in partnership with nonprofits and corporations alike"). Inference: libraries and volunteer networks are not named as delivery channels in the pages opened.

## Open questions for the pod

- Has NSC any position on volunteer-run or library-run AI skills programs as WIOA-eligible providers?
