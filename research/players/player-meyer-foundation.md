**Circle:** field

## Who they are, and where people meet them
A Greater Washington foundation whose program areas are Core Grantmaking, Capacity Building, Rapid Response and the Black Abundance Collaborative, with a 2025–2030 strategic plan aimed at "a region where all can thrive" ([Meyer Foundation blog](https://meyerfoundation.org/blog/)). A Labs organizer would meet it only as an applicant or grantee partner.

## What they bring
- **Financial:** grant totals not stated on the page fetched — **unverified**. The board approved "a strategic increase in the annual payout" ([Meyer blog](https://meyerfoundation.org/blog/)).
- **Social:** Restoration Fund sabbaticals for organizers and frontline staff of grantee partners; a Meeting the Movement Fund with infrastructure and wellness support ([Meyer blog](https://meyerfoundation.org/blog/)).

## What they want
Racial and economic justice, community-centric power building and a "strong local multiracial democracy" ([Meyer blog](https://meyerfoundation.org/blog/)).

## What they've tried about this problem, and how it went
- **Rapid Response grants** for grantee partners (launched 4 Feb, year per page) ([Meyer blog](https://meyerfoundation.org/blog/)).
- Federal workers, AI, workforce training: not mentioned on the page fetched.

## How they see the problem
Power-building and organizer sustainability rather than skills (blog). Inference: a Labs volunteer/organizer burnout question would fit its vocabulary better than an upskilling pitch.
