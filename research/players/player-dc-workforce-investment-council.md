# Player: DC Workforce Investment Council (agency)

**Circle:** field
**Scope:** regional
**Role in the problem:** standard-setter | policy | funder
**Logged:** 2026-11-06

## Who they are, and where people meet them
The District's state workforce board under WIOA. Its board oversees the four-year WIOA Unified State Plan; staff run business engagement, policy guidance, Individual Training Accounts and the Eligible Training Provider list, and sector partnerships ([WIC FY2025 performance plan, 3 Apr 2024](https://oca.dc.gov/sites/default/files/dc/sites/oca/page_content/attachments/WIC_2024-04-05.pdf)). The current plan covers PY2024–2028 with a mid-cycle update open for comment ([DCWorks WIOA State Plan](https://dcworks.dc.gov/page/wioa-state-plan)). A Labs participant would not meet WIC directly; a Labs organizer would meet it when seeking provider status or sector-partnership money.

## What they bring
- **Political / financial:** gatekeeper for WIOA Title I training dollars via ITAs and the ETP list; workers completing sectoral job training rose from 10 (FY2022) to 501 (FY2023) ([WIC plan](https://oca.dc.gov/sites/default/files/dc/sites/oca/page_content/attachments/WIC_2024-04-05.pdf)).
- Eligible training providers counted: 20 in FY2022, 0 in FY2023 (workload measure as reported) ([WIC plan](https://oca.dc.gov/sites/default/files/dc/sites/oca/page_content/attachments/WIC_2024-04-05.pdf)).

## What they want
The State Plan page says the aim is to help job seekers, including youth and people with significant barriers, into "high-quality jobs" and to help employers hire and retain ([DCWorks](https://dcworks.dc.gov/page/wioa-state-plan)).

## What they've tried about this problem, and how it went
- **Sector partnerships** and a demand-occupation list ([DCWorks demand occupation list](https://dcworks.dc.gov/page/demand-occupation-list), search result).
- Public criticism: a 2024 Street Sense piece reported public calls for more oversight of the WIOA program ([Street Sense](https://streetsensemedia.org/article/dc-wioa-workforce-investment-opportunity-wic/), search result; not fetched — **unverified**).
- No AI or library initiatives found on pages fetched.

## How they see the problem
Alignment of training supply with employer demand, measured by business engagement counts and sectoral training completions (performance plan). Inference: a provider-and-credential frame.
