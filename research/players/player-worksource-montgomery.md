# Player: WorkSource Montgomery (agency)

**Circle:** field
**Scope:** regional
**Role in the problem:** referrer | partner project | funder
**Logged:** 2026-11-06

## Who they are, and where people meet them
Montgomery County, Maryland's workforce development organization, operating the American Job Center in Wheaton ([WTOP, Sep 2025](https://wtop.com/montgomery-county/2025/09/displaced-federal-workers-and-contractors-will-find-help-at-a-job-hub-in-montgomery-county)). A displaced federal worker in Montgomery County would meet them at the Mobilize Montgomery Federal Workforce Career Center inside that job center.

## What they bring
- **Financial / built:** $700,000 in county money for the Mobilize Montgomery center, expected to open to the public by early October 2025 ([WTOP](https://wtop.com/montgomery-county/2025/09/displaced-federal-workers-and-contractors-will-find-help-at-a-job-hub-in-montgomery-county)).
- **Context:** about 74,000 federal civilian workers lived in the county and about 48,500 federal civilian jobs were based there before the layoffs ([WTOP](https://wtop.com/montgomery-county/2025/09/displaced-federal-workers-and-contractors-will-find-help-at-a-job-hub-in-montgomery-county)).
- FY2025 annual report exists but could not be fetched ([WSM FY2025 Annual Report](https://worksourcemontgomery.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/WSM-FY2025-Annual-Report-1.pdf)) — budget and totals **unverified**.

## What they want
A WorkSource official said of federal workers with specialized skills, "We can help encourage a lot of these folks... to consider entrepreneurship" ([WTOP](https://wtop.com/montgomery-county/2025/09/displaced-federal-workers-and-contractors-will-find-help-at-a-job-hub-in-montgomery-county)).

## What they've tried about this problem, and how it went
- **Dedicated federal-worker center (2025):** occupational guidance, entrepreneurship encouragement, employer matching ([WTOP](https://wtop.com/montgomery-county/2025/09/displaced-federal-workers-and-contractors-will-find-help-at-a-job-hub-in-montgomery-county); [MyMCM](https://www.mymcmedia.org/worksource-montgomery-adds-center-for-displaced-federal-workers/), search result). Numbers served not yet published on pages fetched.
- AI or peer-cohort programming: none found.

## How they see the problem
Specialized federal skills need re-routing into private employment or self-employment, through a physical hub (WTOP). Inference: a hub-and-counselor frame.
