# Player: Lumina Foundation (funder)

**Circle:** field
**Scope:** national
**Role in the problem:** funder, standard-setter
**Logged:** 2026-11-06

## Who they are, and where people meet them
A national foundation focused on postsecondary attainment. On March 4, 2025 it set a 2040 goal: 75% of working-age adults (25–64) in the workforce holding a degree or credential that leads to "economic prosperity," defined as earning at least 15% more than the national average for high-school-only adults; baseline 44.1% ([Higher Ed Dive](https://www.highereddive.com/news/lumina-sets-new-postsecondary-attainment-goal-75-percent-2040-roi/741513/)). People meet it through state attainment goals, Credential Engine (which it has funded; see existing case), and credential-quality research.

## What they bring
- Cultural/political: the "credential of value" yardstick now used by Gates and by states; prior goal (2008) raised some-college attainment from 38% to 55% ([Higher Ed Dive](https://www.highereddive.com/news/lumina-sets-new-postsecondary-attainment-goal-75-percent-2040-roi/741513/)).
- Financial: endowment and annual grantmaking not stated in the source opened (unverified).

## What they want
75% by 2040, measured through US Census data, with broader well-being measures later ([Higher Ed Dive](https://www.highereddive.com/news/lumina-sets-new-postsecondary-attainment-goal-75-percent-2040-roi/741513/)).

## What they've tried about this problem, and how it went
- Attainment-goal campaigns by state; the 2008 goal (60% by 2025) was not met on its original definition, and the new goal changed the definition to ROI-weighted credentials ([Higher Ed Dive](https://www.highereddive.com/news/lumina-sets-new-postsecondary-attainment-goal-75-percent-2040-roi/741513/)).

## How they see the problem
Frame: the credential is the unit; a route counts when it ends in a credential with measurable earnings return. Inference: a non-credentialed Build Cycle is outside the numerator unless mapped to something countable.

## Open questions for the pod
- Does Lumina count non-degree, non-licensed evidence (a maintained project) anywhere in its measure?
- Which DC/MD/VA attainment coalitions use Lumina's definition?
