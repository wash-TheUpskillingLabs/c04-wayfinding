# Player: A. James & Alice B. Clark Foundation (funder — sunset)

**Circle:** field
**Scope:** regional
**Role in the problem:** funder (former)
**Logged:** 2026-11-06

## Who they are, and where people meet them
A DC family foundation that spent down from $1.4 billion to zero over 2016–2025, investing $592 million in engineering education, $455 million in DC initiatives and $176 million in veterans' programs across 300+ organizations ([Clark Foundation](https://clarkfoundationdc.org/)). A Labs participant would now meet it only through its surviving grantees (e.g., Clark Scholars programs at 11 universities, veterans' career-navigation partners).

## What they bring
- **Financial:** none going forward; the foundation describes its "10-year journey" to $0 ([Clark Foundation](https://clarkfoundationdc.org/)). Inside Philanthropy reported the spend-down approaching $1.3 billion in June 2024 ([Inside Philanthropy](https://david-callahan-hfwl.squarespace.com/home/2024/6/11/nearing-the-end-of-a-10-year-sunset-this-family-foundation-will-have-awarded-13-billion), search result; not fetched).
- **Legacy:** Clark Scholars supports 475 scholars per year "in perpetuity"; veterans' initiatives reported 243,000 transition services including 127,000 career-navigation services ([Clark Foundation](https://clarkfoundationdc.org/)).

## What they want
Mission was to give individuals and families access to opportunities so they could reach their "full potential" ([Clark Foundation](https://clarkfoundationdc.org/)).

## What they've tried about this problem, and how it went
- **DC Initiatives:** ~200 organizations funded, birth-through-college pathways; no adult workforce program named on the site ([Clark Foundation](https://clarkfoundationdc.org/)).
- **Veterans:** large-scale career-navigation funding, which is the closest analogue to mid-career transition work in the region ([Clark Foundation](https://clarkfoundationdc.org/)).

## How they see the problem
Opportunity access through scholarships, pathways and navigation services; now closed (site). Field condition: one of the region's largest workforce-adjacent funders has left the field as of 2025.

## Open questions for the pod
- Which former Clark grantees in DC are now looking for replacement funding, and are any of them potential hosts or partners?
- Did the veterans' career-navigation work leave any reusable method or tooling?
