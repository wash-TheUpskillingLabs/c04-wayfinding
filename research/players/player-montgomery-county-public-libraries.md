# Player: Montgomery County Public Libraries (institution)

**Circle:** field
**Scope:** regional
**Role in the problem:** host | referrer | peer program
**Logged:** 2026-11-06

## Who they are, and where people meet them
Montgomery County, Maryland's 22-branch public library system ([Report to the Community FY2025](https://www.montgomerycountymd.gov/library/resources/files/about/report-to-community-2025.pdf)). A Labs participant in suburban Maryland would meet MCPL at a branch workforce program (résumé clinics, career coaching, mock interviews) or a digital-skills class.

## What they bring
- **Financial / built:** FY2025 budget $62.8 million; 22 branches; 3.68 million visits ([FY2025 report](https://www.montgomerycountymd.gov/library/resources/files/about/report-to-community-2025.pdf)).
- **Human / social:** 12,032 programs with 329,692 attendees in FY2025, including 496 workforce programs with 8,186 attendees ([FY2025 report](https://www.montgomerycountymd.gov/library/resources/files/about/report-to-community-2025.pdf)).

## What they want
The FY2025 report describes workforce offerings "with a focus on federal employees affected by workforce cuts," plus Career Online High School (free accredited diploma) and the Senior Planet digital-skills series ([FY2025 report](https://www.montgomerycountymd.gov/library/resources/files/about/report-to-community-2025.pdf)).

## What they've tried about this problem, and how it went
- **Workforce programming at scale:** 496 programs / 8,186 attendees in one year, roughly 16 attendees per session (calculated) ([FY2025 report](https://www.montgomerycountymd.gov/library/resources/files/about/report-to-community-2025.pdf)).
- **Federal-worker pivot in 2025:** résumé clinics, LinkedIn reviews, free headshots aimed at displaced federal employees ([FY2025 report](https://www.montgomerycountymd.gov/library/resources/files/about/report-to-community-2025.pdf)).
- **Earlier "Road to Success" workforce programs** are documented by the Urban Libraries Council ([ULC](https://www.urbanlibraries.org/innovations/road-to-success-workforce-development-programs)) — not fetched in full; unverified detail.
- Makerspaces and AI programming: not mentioned in the FY2025 report.

## How they see the problem
Workforce support is framed as discrete services (clinic, coaching, diploma), reported by attendance counts; no published pathway or outcome measure beyond attendance ([FY2025 report](https://www.montgomerycountymd.gov/library/resources/files/about/report-to-community-2025.pdf)). Inference: service-delivery frame, not cohort or community frame.

## Open questions for the pod
- Does MCPL count repeat attendees, and does it know what people do after a clinic?
- Is there appetite for a cohort model in branches, given the 2025 federal-worker demand?
