# Player: Microsoft Elevate (funder)

**Circle:** field
**Scope:** national (global program)
**Role in the problem:** funder, standard-setter (credentials), partner project (with PLA)
**Logged:** 2026-11-06

## Who they are, and where people meet them
Microsoft's successor to Microsoft Philanthropies and Tech for Social Impact, announced July 9, 2025, with a pledge of more than $4 billion over five years in cash, AI and cloud technology to K-12 schools, community and technical colleges and nonprofits; the Elevate Academy aims to help 20 million people earn an AI credential within two years ([Microsoft On the Issues](https://blogs.microsoft.com/on-the-issues/2025/07/09/elevate/)). A Labs person is most likely to meet it through its October 2026 partnership with PLA, "Building AI Skills and Agency Through Libraries," which gives free AI training and webinars to library workers and patrons ([Route Fifty](https://www.route-fifty.com/artificial-intelligence/2026/10/microsoft-help-public-libraries-build-ai-literacy-communities/416817/)).

## What they bring
- Financial: $4B+ over five years, worldwide ([Microsoft](https://blogs.microsoft.com/on-the-issues/2025/07/09/elevate/)).
- Cultural/human: credential ladders "from foundational to advanced"; partnerships with AFT (National Academy for AI Instruction), AFL-CIO, Code.org ("Hour of AI"); an AI Economy Institute as its research arm ([Microsoft](https://blogs.microsoft.com/on-the-issues/2025/07/09/elevate/)).
- For libraries: a community of practice and planned case studies with PLA ([Route Fifty](https://www.route-fifty.com/artificial-intelligence/2026/10/microsoft-help-public-libraries-build-ai-literacy-communities/416817/)).

## What they want
"Putting people first" (post title); counted outcomes are credentials earned, 20 million in two years ([Microsoft](https://blogs.microsoft.com/on-the-issues/2025/07/09/elevate/)).

## What they've tried about this problem, and how it went
- The PLA partnership is new (Oct 22, 2026); no figures for participating libraries, dollars or people were published ([Route Fifty](https://www.route-fifty.com/artificial-intelligence/2026/10/microsoft-help-public-libraries-build-ai-literacy-communities/416817/)).
- Progress toward the 20M credential target was not found in sources opened.

## How they see the problem
Frame: a credential-count problem solved by free content plus institutional partners (unions, schools, libraries). Inference: the library is a distribution channel for Microsoft content; the local program's own method is not in the frame.
