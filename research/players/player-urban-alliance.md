# Player: Urban Alliance (program)

**Circle:** field
**Scope:** national (DC-born)
**Role in the problem:** peer program | referrer
**Logged:** 2026-11-06

## Who they are, and where people meet them
A DC-founded nonprofit running a paid nine-month program for high-school seniors: professional-development training, a paid internship and one-on-one support; the Greater DC region typically serves 200+ students from 35+ high schools with 100+ businesses, and the organization reports 4,000+ interns over 25 years and 18,000+ reached through workshops ([Urban Alliance DC](https://theurbanalliance.org/location/washington-dc/)). A Labs participant would meet it only as an alumnus or as a partner employer's intern.

## What they bring
- **Social:** 200+ employer partners nationally; Smithsonian and Bank of America named for an arts internship track ([Urban Alliance DC](https://theurbanalliance.org/location/washington-dc/)).
- **Cultural (evidence):** an Urban Institute randomized trial of 1,062 DC and Baltimore students ([Next City, 29 Aug 2017](https://nextcity.org/daily/entry/evaluation-youth-internship-programs-succeed)).

## What they want
Site language: level the playing field for young people in the workforce ([Urban Alliance DC](https://theurbanalliance.org/location/washington-dc/)).

## What they've tried about this problem, and how it went
- **RCT results:** college enrollment 69% vs 64% control; completing a year of college 52% vs 45%; two-year degree or third-year enrollment 28% vs 21%; Next City's reading was that internships "didn't significantly increase" college attendance ([Next City](https://nextcity.org/daily/entry/evaluation-youth-internship-programs-succeed)). A later DOL CLEAR-listed evaluation (Theodos et al., 2023) exists but redirected and was not read — **unverified**.
- **Federal pilot:** Urban Alliance was selected for a federal-government jobs pilot ([Urban Alliance](https://theurbanalliance.org/urban-alliance-selected-for-federal-government-jobs-pilot/), search result; not fetched).
- No published alumni-network or post-program route on the DC page.

## How they see the problem
Work experience plus coaching during the last year of school changes post-secondary trajectories, modestly (RCT). Inference: a youth-transition frame with rigorous measurement, unusual in the field.

## Open questions for the pod
- What does Urban Alliance do with alumni after the internship, and does any alumni community persist?
- Could its employer partners be the same firms that would host Labs showcases?
