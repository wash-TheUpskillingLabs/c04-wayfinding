# Player: Fairfax County Public Library (institution)

**Circle:** field
**Scope:** regional
**Role in the problem:** host | referrer
**Logged:** 2026-11-06

## Who they are, and where people meet them
Fairfax County, Virginia's public library system. The county's Digital Equity and Inclusion page (part of One Fairfax) lists library "Tech Tutors" sessions and library courses alongside the county's Community Technology Program and Technology Education for Older Adults ([Fairfax County technology classes](https://www.fairfaxcounty.gov/topics/digital-equity/technology-classes)). A Labs participant would meet FCPL through a Tech Tutor slot or an "Ask Your Library" calendar event.

## What they bring
- **Built / human:** branch network and a library event calendar with recurring tech help events ([Fairfax library calendar example](https://librarycalendar.fairfaxcounty.gov/event/15324131)). Branch count, budget and attendance were not on pages fetched — **unverified**.
- **Political:** sits under the county's One Fairfax digital-equity framing ([Fairfax County](https://www.fairfaxcounty.gov/topics/digital-equity/technology-classes)).

## What they want
The county page describes "classes, trainings and support opportunities for residents to advance digital literacy skills" ([Fairfax County](https://www.fairfaxcounty.gov/topics/digital-equity/technology-classes)).

## What they've tried about this problem, and how it went
- **Tech Tutors** (one-to-one help) via the library calendar; topics and outcomes not published on the page fetched.
- Makerspace, cohort, or AI programming: none found in this pass. Workforce support for federal workers in Fairfax in 2025 was routed through the Economic Development Authority's job database rather than the library ([WTOP, Feb 2025](https://wtop.com/local/2025/02/here-are-the-resources-available-in-each-dc-area-county-to-fired-federal-workers)).

## How they see the problem
Digital literacy as an equity service, split between the library and Neighborhood & Community Services (inference from page structure).
