# Player: Federal City Council (network)

**Circle:** field
**Scope:** regional
**Role in the problem:** policy | partner project
**Logged:** 2026-11-06

## Who they are, and where people meet them
A membership body of DC business and civic leaders describing itself as "A Catalyst for Progress in the Nation's Capital," with initiatives in District Strong, Transportation & Infrastructure, Education and Workforce, Union Station, Public Safety and an Emerging Leaders Program ([Federal City Council](https://www.federalcitycouncil.org/)). A Labs participant would rarely meet it directly; a Labs organizer might meet it through its Education and Workforce work or its leaders program.

## What they want
Site language: Education and Workforce "supports school facilities and employment outcomes for students" ([Federal City Council](https://www.federalcitycouncil.org/)).
