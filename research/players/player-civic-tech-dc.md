# Player: Civic Tech DC, formerly Code for DC (network)

**Circle:** field
**Scope:** regional
**Role in the problem:** peer program | partner project
**Logged:** 2026-11-06

## Who they are, and where people meet them
A volunteer civic-tech meetup founded in 2012 that renamed itself Civic Tech DC in October 2023 after Code for America required brigades to drop the "Code for" name; it meets monthly, with 30–40 attendees per meetup from August 2023, and found a fiscal sponsor in the Open Collective Foundation after Code for America cut financial ties in February 2023 ([Technical.ly, 2 Nov 2023](https://technical.ly/civic-news/code-for-dc-rebrand-civic-tech-dc.md)). A Labs participant who wants to keep building after a Cycle would meet them at a meetup ([Meetup](https://www.meetup.com/civic-tech-dc/)).

## What they bring
- **Social / human:** a standing volunteer community of developers and designers; projects including a DC tool-lending library, a case-management system for abortion funds, and an ANC/elected-official lookup ([Technical.ly](https://technical.ly/civic-news/code-for-dc-rebrand-civic-tech-dc.md)).
- **Financial:** minimal; fiscal sponsorship only ([Technical.ly](https://technical.ly/civic-news/code-for-dc-rebrand-civic-tech-dc.md)).
- The group maintains its own history page ([Civic Tech DC history](https://civictechdc.netlify.app/history/), search result; not fetched).

## What they want
Organizers said the new name is "more clear about what we're doing" ([Technical.ly](https://technical.ly/civic-news/code-for-dc-rebrand-civic-tech-dc.md)); the aim is building tools for the public good with volunteers.

## What they've tried about this problem, and how it went
- **Loss of national hub:** the brigade network's parent withdrew support in 2023; the group lost access to CfA resources and connections to other brigades but kept meeting ([Technical.ly](https://technical.ly/civic-news/code-for-dc-rebrand-civic-tech-dc.md)). This is a direct precedent for a volunteer network outliving its hub.
- **Cadence decay and recovery:** had met twice monthly, dropped to monthly, and organizers wanted to return to twice monthly ([Technical.ly](https://technical.ly/civic-news/code-for-dc-rebrand-civic-tech-dc.md)).
- **Project maintenance:** long-running projects persist across years (tool library, DARIA); no published data on how many projects are abandoned.

## How they see the problem
Volunteer projects need a recurring room and a fiscal home; identity and name matter for recruitment (rebrand coverage). Inference: a maintainer-community frame, the closest regional analogue to The Labs' post-cycle "maintained project" route.

## Open questions for the pod
- How do newcomers at a Civic Tech DC meetup pick a project, and how many come back a second time?
- Would they co-host a Share workshop or absorb Labs projects as maintained work?
