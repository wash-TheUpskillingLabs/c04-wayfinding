# Player: National Digital Inclusion Alliance, NDIA (network)

**Circle:** field
**Scope:** national
**Role in the problem:** peer program, standard-setter (digital navigator model), policy
**Logged:** 2026-11-06

## Who they are, and where people meet them
The national alliance of digital-inclusion practitioners (libraries, nonprofits, local governments) and the originator of the "digital navigator" role. Its executive director is Angela Siefer ([Cabling Installation & Maintenance, Oct 16 2025](https://www.cablinginstall.com/connectivity/news/55323923/ndia-sues-federal-government-to-restore-digital-equity-act-grant-funding)). People meet it through affiliate programs, its Net Inclusion conference and digital-navigator training; DC Public Library's digital-navigator-type staff would know its materials.

## What they bring
- Social/built: a Digital Navigator+ program design: 13 local navigator programs in 11 states, about 30,000 people to be assisted, 6,500 wireless accounts and devices ([same](https://www.cablinginstall.com/connectivity/news/55323923/ndia-sues-federal-government-to-restore-digital-equity-act-grant-funding)).
- Financial: a $25.7M Digital Equity Act competitive grant awarded early 2025 and terminated May 9, 2025, the day after the President called the Act "RACIST" on social media; NDIA sued in October 2025 in DDC with the Lawyers' Committee for Civil Rights Under Law ([Smart Cities Dive, Nov 13 2025](https://www.smartcitiesdive.com/news/trump-digital-equity-act-lawsuit-dei/805419/)). Affiliate count not found (unverified).

## What they want
Restoration of the Digital Equity Act's $2.75B program and continuation of navigator services; its legal argument is separation of powers ([Smart Cities Dive](https://www.smartcitiesdive.com/news/trump-digital-equity-act-lawsuit-dei/805419/)).

## What they've tried about this problem, and how it went
- The digital navigator: a one-to-one, trusted-person model for the first threshold (getting online, first device, first skills). The 2025 program was stopped before it ran at scale; local programs in Pennsylvania and North Carolina were canceled ([PublicSource](https://www.publicsource.org/pennsylvania-digital-equity-grants-terminated/); [WUNC](https://www.wunc.org/2025-11-12/how-ending-the-digital-equity-act-has-disrupted-programs-to-help-people-get-online), search results; not opened).

## How they see the problem
Frame: access and a trusted human guide come before skills; the navigator is the on-ramp. Inference: AI skills are a later step in its ladder, and funding for the first step is what collapsed in 2025.
