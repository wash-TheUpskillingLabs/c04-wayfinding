# Player: DC Department of Employment Services (agency)

**Circle:** field
**Scope:** regional
**Role in the problem:** referrer | funder | policy | employer
**Logged:** 2026-11-06

## Who they are, and where people meet them
The District's labor and workforce agency; it runs the American Job Centers, unemployment insurance, apprenticeship and the DC Infrastructure Academy ([DOES oversight testimony, 26 Feb 2026](https://dccouncil.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/DOES-2026-Performance-Oversight-Hearing-Testimony_2.26.2026vF.pdf)). A Labs participant who is a displaced federal worker most likely met DOES first, at an unemployment claim or an American Job Center, before finding a library cohort.

## What they bring
- **Human / financial:** FY2025 American Job Centers served nearly 32,000 unique participants and delivered 77,000+ career services; 37,000+ initial UI claims adjudicated and $172 million+ paid in FY2025 ([DOES testimony](https://dccouncil.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/DOES-2026-Performance-Oversight-Hearing-Testimony_2.26.2026vF.pdf)).
- **Built:** DC Infrastructure Academy, which has trained 5,300+ since 2018 with a reported 69% employment rate, with a Spingarn campus opening in 2026 ([DOES testimony](https://dccouncil.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/DOES-2026-Performance-Oversight-Hearing-Testimony_2.26.2026vF.pdf)).
- **Political:** 319 apprenticeship sponsors; registered employers 396 new in FY2025 ([DOES testimony](https://dccouncil.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/DOES-2026-Performance-Oversight-Hearing-Testimony_2.26.2026vF.pdf)).

## What they want
Across adult training programs DOES reports "over 50%" of participants gaining unsubsidized employment; the testimony's measures are placements, certifications and employer engagement ([DOES testimony](https://dccouncil.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/DOES-2026-Performance-Oversight-Hearing-Testimony_2.26.2026vF.pdf)).

## What they've tried about this problem, and how it went
- **Federal-worker response:** "Gateway to District Careers" federal hiring event (March 2025) with 60+ agencies and employers; participation in the regional talentcapital.ai marketplace; a dislocated-worker page ([DOES testimony](https://dccouncil.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/DOES-2026-Performance-Oversight-Hearing-Testimony_2.26.2026vF.pdf); [Dislocated worker](https://does.dc.gov/service/dislocated-worker)). No federal-worker-specific outcome counts are published in the testimony.
- **AI:** launched what it calls the nation's first registered apprenticeship for "AI Data Annotator" in May 2025; no enrollment figure given ([DOES testimony](https://dccouncil.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/DOES-2026-Performance-Oversight-Hearing-Testimony_2.26.2026vF.pdf)).
- **Libraries:** not mentioned in the 2026 testimony.

## How they see the problem
Job seekers move through a pipeline of services to placement; progress is counted in services delivered and employment rate (testimony). Inference: a case-management frame; peer cohorts and volunteer networks do not appear.

## Open questions for the pod
- Do American Job Center staff know about library cohorts, and would they refer?
- Could Labs participation count toward anything in DOES's Eligible Training Provider or ITA system, or is that a different world entirely?
