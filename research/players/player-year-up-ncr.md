# Player: Year Up United — National Capital Region (program)

**Circle:** field
**Scope:** national (regional site)
**Role in the problem:** peer program | competitor
**Logged:** 2026-11-06

## Who they are, and where people meet them
The DC site (609 H St NE, Suite 500, with Woodbridge, VA also listed) of a national young-adult training-and-internship nonprofit; tracks include Application Development, Data Analytics, IT Support, Network Security, Project Management, Banking and Business Operations; the learning phase is in person 3–4 days a week; next starts listed for Spring 2027 ([Year Up NCR](https://www.yearup.org/locations/national-capital-region)). A younger Labs participant might meet them as an applicant; a Labs organizer as a peer.

## What they bring
- **Social:** listed potential internship partners Bank of America, JPMorgan Chase, LinkedIn, Salesforce, Amazon ([Year Up NCR](https://www.yearup.org/locations/national-capital-region)).
- Program length, stipend, eligibility ages and outcomes are not on the NCR page — **unverified** here.

## What they want
Not stated on the NCR page beyond track and partner lists.

## What they've tried about this problem, and how it went
- **Corporate-internship on-ramp** rather than open community; also offers a "National Capital Region – Virtual Campus" ([Year Up virtual campus](https://yearup.org/locations/national-capital-region-virtual-campus), search result).
- Job-board syndication: Year Up advertises its training tracks as job postings on Monster, Snagajob and similar boards ([Monster listing](https://www.monster.com/job-openings/sales-fundamentals-career-training-program-washington-dc--cbaf28d7-99ea-4e55-bec2-bd41cf8e6806), search result) — an on-ramp placed where job seekers already look.

## How they see the problem
Young adults need a bridge to corporate employers via structured training plus a paid internship (site). Inference: employer-sponsored pipeline; mid-career federal workers are out of scope.
