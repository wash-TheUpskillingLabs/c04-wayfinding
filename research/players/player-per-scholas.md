# Player: Per Scholas (institution)

**Circle:** field
**Scope:** national
**Role in the problem:** peer program, competitor (for learners), employer partner
**Logged:** 2026-11-06

## Who they are, and where people meet them
A 30-year-old tuition-free tech training nonprofit, "scaling access across 25 cities," training 5,555+ people in 2025, with a Career Accelerator for alumni (2,650+ upskilled in 2025; 7,000+ enrollments since 2021) and 1,110+ employer partners ([2025 Annual Report, July 2026](https://perscholas.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Per-Scholas-Annual-Report-2026.pdf)). People meet it as applicants to a full-time course, as alumni, or as employers. A National Capital Region campus is not named in the report (Per Scholas has had a DC-area site; not verified here).

## What they bring
- Financial: 2025 revenue $81.65M, expenses $79.10M, net assets $45.93M; 80% to programs ([Annual Report](https://perscholas.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Per-Scholas-Annual-Report-2026.pdf)).
- Human: AI tracks organized as General AI, Generative AI, Domain-Specific AI (embedded in Cybersecurity and Salesforce) and Agentic AI; an "AI-native classroom model" in development ([Annual Report](https://perscholas.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Per-Scholas-Annual-Report-2026.pdf)).

## What they want
Access and wage outcomes: data-center technicians average $53K, about 3x pre-training income; average wage growth 2.5x; 81% job attainment within 12 months for data-center graduates ([Annual Report](https://perscholas.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Per-Scholas-Annual-Report-2026.pdf)).

## What they've tried about this problem, and how it went
- Post-program route: the Career Accelerator (alumni upskilling) shows a 14.7% average wage increase 7–12 months after training ([Annual Report](https://perscholas.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Per-Scholas-Annual-Report-2026.pdf)). This is the clearest published "what comes after" structure among the peer programs here.
- Expansion by satellite partnerships (LA, Cincinnati, Philadelphia, Pittsburgh in 2025) rather than new campuses ([Annual Report](https://perscholas.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Per-Scholas-Annual-Report-2026.pdf)).

## How they see the problem
Frame: a cohort course leading to a job, then an alumni upskilling loop; employers are the demand signal (nine of the top ten data-center markets). Inference: the on-ramp is an application and selection, not a drop-in.
