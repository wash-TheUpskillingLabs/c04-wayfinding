# Player: Alliance of Civic Technologists, ACT (network)

**Circle:** field
**Scope:** national
**Role in the problem:** peer program, partner project
**Logged:** 2026-11-06

## Who they are, and where people meet them
A decentralized network of civic-tech volunteer groups founded in 2023 to fill the gap left by Code for America's Brigade Network; organized initially by former brigade program manager Christopher Whitaker ([civictech.guide](https://civictech.guide/projects/alliance-of-civic-technologists-act); [StateScoop](https://statescoop.com/code-for-america-former-brigades-regroup/)). People meet it through member groups (e.g. Chi Hack Night joined Dec 2024, [blog](https://chihacknight.org/blog/2024/12/16/2024-joining-act)), its Discord, and an ACT Congress (a 2025 press release on the inaugural Congress exists but could not be opened; unverified).

## What they bring
- Financial: very little. Its Open Collective fund shows $6,905.03 raised and the same disbursed, from 25 contributors ([Open Collective](https://opencollective.com/act-fund)).
- Social: launched with 16 cities in talks and letters of intent from former brigades in Seattle, Dayton, Boston and Oakland ([Open Collective](https://opencollective.com/act-fund); [StateScoop](https://statescoop.com/code-for-america-former-brigades-regroup/)). Current member count not found.
- Each member group must find its own fiscal sponsor; many moved to Open Collective Foundation ([StateScoop](https://statescoop.com/code-for-america-former-brigades-regroup/)).

## What they want
Continuity and growth of civic-tech volunteer communities, and advocacy for technology and policy changes that improve public-service delivery ([civictech.guide](https://civictech.guide/projects/alliance-of-civic-technologists-act)). It "will not build products" ([StateScoop](https://statescoop.com/code-for-america-former-brigades-regroup/)).

## What they've tried about this problem, and how it went
- Replaced a centrally staffed, fiscally sponsored network with a co-created, Discord-based one; the result is far thinner infrastructure than before (no fiscal sponsorship, no annual conference at launch) ([StateScoop](https://statescoop.com/code-for-america-former-brigades-regroup/)).
- No published data on volunteer onboarding or retention across member groups was found.

## How they see the problem
Frame: stability of local groups first ("brigades need to be able to be stable"), then shared voice. Inference: it is a federation of peers rather than a program with on-ramps; wayfinding happens inside each local group.
