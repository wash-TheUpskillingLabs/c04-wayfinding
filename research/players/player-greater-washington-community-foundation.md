# Player: Greater Washington Community Foundation (funder)

**Circle:** field
**Scope:** regional
**Role in the problem:** funder | partner project
**Logged:** 2026-11-06

## Who they are, and where people meet them
The region's community foundation (DC, Montgomery, Prince George's, Northern Virginia). In 2025 it deployed more than $14.3 million in grants and technical assistance, ran a Solidarity Fund for former USAID workers, seeded Talent Capital AI and the DMV Monitor dashboard, and co-convened "Greater Washington Together" with government, employer, education and nonprofit leaders ([GWCF 2025 review](https://www.thecommunityfoundation.org/news/a-year-in-review-looking-back-at-the-top-milestones-from-2025)). A Labs organizer meets it as a grant-maker and convener; a participant might meet its funded pilots (Thrive Prince George's, MoCo Boost guaranteed income).

## What they bring
- **Financial:** $14.3 million+ deployed in 2025; the "Together, We Prosper" campaign raised $78 million from 1,200+ donors; Bridge Grants of $1 million+ to 21 organizations facing funding losses ([GWCF 2025 review](https://www.thecommunityfoundation.org/news/a-year-in-review-looking-back-at-the-top-milestones-from-2025)).
- **Social / political:** convenor of Greater Washington Together ([GWCF 2025 review](https://www.thecommunityfoundation.org/news/a-year-in-review-looking-back-at-the-top-milestones-from-2025)).

## What they want
Stated 2025 priorities: nonprofit capacity and resilience; economic mobility including "closing the racial wealth gap"; ending homelessness; health and housing; food security; protecting DC self-governance ([GWCF 2025 review](https://www.thecommunityfoundation.org/news/a-year-in-review-looking-back-at-the-top-milestones-from-2025)). A 2024 release announced $10 million in economic-mobility grants in DC ([GWCF release](https://www.thecommunityfoundation.org/news/greater-washington-community-foundation-announces-10-million-in-grants-to-foster-economic-mobility-in-dc), search result; not fetched).

## What they've tried about this problem, and how it went
- **Federal-worker response (2025):** cash assistance (Solidarity Fund), seed money for Talent Capital AI, and a data dashboard — i.e., relief, matching and measurement rather than training ([GWCF 2025 review](https://www.thecommunityfoundation.org/news/a-year-in-review-looking-back-at-the-top-milestones-from-2025)).
- **Economic mobility pilots:** guaranteed income and savings pilots in Montgomery and Prince George's ([GWCF 2025 review](https://www.thecommunityfoundation.org/news/a-year-in-review-looking-back-at-the-top-milestones-from-2025)).
- Direct funding of library-based or volunteer-run upskilling: none found in this pass.

## How they see the problem
Economic mobility and sector resilience, addressed with cash, data and regional convening (2025 review). Inference: a systems-and-equity funder that backs platforms and pilots more than programs.
