# Player: Prince George's County Memorial Library System (institution)

**Circle:** field
**Scope:** regional
**Role in the problem:** host | referrer | partner project
**Logged:** 2026-11-06

## Who they are, and where people meet them
The public library system for Prince George's County, Maryland. Its Workforce and Career Development (WCD) service has its own hub page ([PGCMLS WCD](https://pgcmls.libnet.info/WCD/WCD-home)). A Labs participant would meet PGCMLS at a branch career session or through its partnership with Employ Prince George's.

## What they bring
- **Human / social:** A dedicated workforce development team; Employ Prince George's was certifying library staff as career development professionals, with five staff expected to complete certification in the year of the ULC entry (2020) ([ULC](https://www.urbanlibraries.org/innovations/pgcmls-workforce-development)).
- **Political:** County agencies helped focus the library's workforce resources on high-unemployment areas ([ULC](https://www.urbanlibraries.org/innovations/pgcmls-workforce-development)).
- Branch count and budget not on pages fetched — **unverified**.

## What they want
The 2020 entry states the aim of improving employability for "underemployed, unemployed, and career-changing residents" and becoming a leading county resource for employment needs ([ULC](https://www.urbanlibraries.org/innovations/pgcmls-workforce-development)).

## What they've tried about this problem, and how it went
- **Planned physical workforce space delayed by COVID-19;** work moved online as weekly virtual career sessions for young professionals and short tips for teens ([ULC](https://www.urbanlibraries.org/innovations/pgcmls-workforce-development)).
- **Staff certification via the workforce board** rather than hiring new staff ([ULC](https://www.urbanlibraries.org/innovations/pgcmls-workforce-development)).
- Outcomes on the ULC page are listed as anticipated, not achieved. The current WCD page exists ([PGCMLS WCD](https://pgcmls.libnet.info/WCD/WCD-home)) but its content was not extracted — **unverified**.

## How they see the problem
Employability services for residents, delivered by librarians trained in the workforce system's own credential (ULC entry). Inference: a library-as-workforce-front-door frame, closer to the WIOA system than DCPL's maker frame.
