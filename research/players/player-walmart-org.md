# Player: Walmart.org / Walmart Foundation (funder)

**Circle:** field
**Scope:** national
**Role in the problem:** funder, employer
**Logged:** 2026-11-06

## Who they are, and where people meet them
The philanthropic arm of the largest US private employer. Its "Creating Opportunity" / Retail Opportunity strategy has four pillars: skills-first hiring and advancement, quality data on workers' skills, shifting the narrative toward the business case, and data mobility between systems; grants are by invitation only to 501(c)(3)s and unsolicited proposals are not accepted ([Granted summary of walmart.org](https://grantedai.com/grants/creating-opportunity-walmart-foundation-a36eab4f); source page [walmart.org/what-we-do/creating-opportunity](https://walmart.org/what-we-do/creating-opportunity), not opened). As an employer, Walmart is the launch partner for OpenAI's certifications for its 2 million associates ([Benzinga](https://benzinga.com/markets/tech/25/09/47515310/openai-to-certify-10-million-americans-in-generative-ai-by-2030-partnering-with-walmart-to-train-the-next-wave-of-ai-savvy-workers)).

## What they bring
- Financial: example grants cited include $5M to Rockefeller Philanthropic Inc. and $450K to Skills for Chicagoland's Future; no award limits published ([Granted](https://grantedai.com/grants/creating-opportunity-walmart-foundation-a36eab4f)). Walmart.org has funded Opportunity@Work-style skills-first work (not verified from a Walmart page).
- Employer: 2.1 million US workers to be AI-trained; press reports a message that AI will "improve" jobs rather than remove them ([Fortune, Jun 2026](https://fortune.com/2026/06/12/walmart-message-2-1-million-workers-ai-improve-job-not-take-it-technology-power-future/), not opened).

## What they want
Skills-first systems with portable worker data; "data mobility between systems" is named as a pillar ([Granted](https://grantedai.com/grants/creating-opportunity-walmart-foundation-a36eab4f)).

## What they've tried about this problem, and how it went
- Funds intermediaries that build skills data and LER-type infrastructure (see existing cases in `field-records/`). Outcomes are reported by grantees, not on the pages opened.

## How they see the problem
Frame: a records and signaling problem; if skills data moves between systems, pathways open. Inference: a volunteer network is relevant to it only if its records are portable.
