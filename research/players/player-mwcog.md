# Player: Metropolitan Washington Council of Governments — Talent Capital (network | agency)

**Circle:** field
**Scope:** regional
**Role in the problem:** policy | partner project | standard-setter
**Logged:** 2026-11-06

## Who they are, and where people meet them
The regional association of 24 local governments in DC, Maryland and Virginia ([Virginia Business, 1 Oct 2025](https://virginiabusiness.com/d-c-area-leaders-launch-initiative-to-help-laid-off-federal-workers)). It publishes the Federal Workforce Data Dashboard and, on 1 October 2025, launched Talent Capital (TalentCapital.AI), a regional job and reskilling platform for displaced federal workers operated by DC software firm BuildWithin ([COG economy page](https://www.mwcog.org/community/planning-areas/economy/); [Virginia Business](https://virginiabusiness.com/d-c-area-leaders-launch-initiative-to-help-laid-off-federal-workers)). A displaced federal worker meets COG through Talent Capital's AI agents, job listings and training links; DOES testimony lists talentcapital.ai as a DC channel ([DOES testimony, Feb 2026](https://dccouncil.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/DOES-2026-Performance-Oversight-Hearing-Testimony_2.26.2026vF.pdf)).

## What they bring
- **Political:** backing of all 24 member governments for Talent Capital ([Virginia Business](https://virginiabusiness.com/d-c-area-leaders-launch-initiative-to-help-laid-off-federal-workers)).
- **Cultural (data):** COG's 27 Apr 2026 analysis found the region lost 62,100 federal jobs (−16.5%) and ~103,900 jobs overall from January 2025 to January 2026, the lowest federal count since 1990 ([FFXnow, 28 Apr 2026](https://www.ffxnow.com/2026/04/28/report-d-c-region-has-its-fewest-federal-workers-in-at-least-36-years/)).
- **Financial:** Talent Capital received seed funding from the Greater Washington Community Foundation; amount not stated ([GWCF 2025 review](https://www.thecommunityfoundation.org/news/a-year-in-review-looking-back-at-the-top-milestones-from-2025)).

## What they want
COG's executive director called the numbers "flashing red lights that we need to work together as a region" ([Virginia Business](https://virginiabusiness.com/d-c-area-leaders-launch-initiative-to-help-laid-off-federal-workers)). Talent Capital prioritizes AI, big data, cybersecurity, health care and tourism careers ([Virginia Business](https://virginiabusiness.com/d-c-area-leaders-launch-initiative-to-help-laid-off-federal-workers)).

## What they've tried about this problem, and how it went
- **Talent Capital (Oct 2025–):** consolidated job listings, free AI agents for career navigation and reskilling, links to coaching and events ([Virginia Business](https://virginiabusiness.com/d-c-area-leaders-launch-initiative-to-help-laid-off-federal-workers)). Users or placements: not published on pages fetched. Brookings cited it as an early example of coordinated response ([Brookings, 5 Mar 2026](https://www.brookings.edu/articles/after-the-fork-greater-washington-leads-the-nation-in-regional-job-loss)).
- **Potomac Regional Alliance (Sep 2026):** four organizations formed it to launch a CEDS initiative on long-term competitiveness ([COG economy page](https://www.mwcog.org/community/planning-areas/economy/)).

## How they see the problem
Regional labor-market shock requiring coordinated matching and reskilling, delivered through a digital platform with AI agents (launch coverage). Inference: an interface-first frame — the platform is the on-ramp.
