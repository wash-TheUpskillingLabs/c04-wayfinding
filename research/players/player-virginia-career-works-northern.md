# Player: Virginia Career Works Northern Region / SkillSource Group (agency | network)

**Circle:** field
**Scope:** regional
**Role in the problem:** referrer | funder | partner project
**Logged:** 2026-11-06

## Who they are, and where people meet them
The WIOA workforce system for Northern Virginia: Fairfax, Loudoun and Prince William counties and the cities of Fairfax, Falls Church, Manassas and Manassas Park, working closely with Arlington and Alexandria; SkillSource Group is its nonprofit partner ([Virginia Works, 6 Nov 2025](https://virginiaworks.gov/virginia-works-announces-over-500000-in-additional-funding-to-support-norther-virginia-job-seekers/)). SkillSource Group is a 501(c)(3) (EIN 30-0129320) ([CauseIQ](https://causeiq.com/organizations/skillsource-group,300129320), search result). A Labs participant in Virginia would meet them at a job center or through the NOVAnext scholarship application, which Virginia Career Works reviews ([CC Daily, 13 Mar 2025](https://www.ccdaily.com/2025/03/nova-program-to-help-dislocated-federal-workers-contractors/)).

## What they bring
- **Financial:** Over $500,000 in additional state Rapid Response funding announced 6 Nov 2025 for displaced federal workers and contractors; a separate $43,750 gubernatorial salary donation earlier in 2025 ([Virginia Works](https://virginiaworks.gov/virginia-works-announces-over-500000-in-additional-funding-to-support-norther-virginia-job-seekers/)).
- **Human:** case management, skills assessments, training in IT, cybersecurity, healthcare, project management; free LinkedIn Learning licenses; transportation aid ([Virginia Works](https://virginiaworks.gov/virginia-works-announces-over-500000-in-additional-funding-to-support-norther-virginia-job-seekers/)).

## What they want
The release targets "hundreds of former Federal workers and contractors" with reemployment services, career navigation and training ([Virginia Works](https://virginiaworks.gov/virginia-works-announces-over-500000-in-additional-funding-to-support-norther-virginia-job-seekers/)).

## What they've tried about this problem, and how it went
- **Rapid Response project (2025–26)** for federal workers; numbers served not given beyond "hundreds" ([Virginia Works](https://virginiaworks.gov/virginia-works-announces-over-500000-in-additional-funding-to-support-norther-virginia-job-seekers/)).
- **NOVAnext intake partner** for NOVA's $1 million scholarship fund ([CC Daily](https://www.ccdaily.com/2025/03/nova-program-to-help-dislocated-federal-workers-contractors/)).
- AI-specific upskilling: none found beyond LinkedIn Learning licenses.

## How they see the problem
Dislocated workers need navigation, credentials and supportive services, delivered by case managers (release). Inference: the same case-management frame as DOES, with licensed e-learning as the self-directed layer.

## Open questions for the pod
- Where do Virginia case managers send someone who wants a peer group rather than a certification?
- Does the LinkedIn Learning license get used, and what does "completion" look like for them?
