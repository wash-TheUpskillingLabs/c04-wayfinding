# Player: Northern Virginia Community College — Workforce / NOVAnext (institution)

**Circle:** field
**Scope:** regional
**Role in the problem:** peer program | referrer | partner project
**Logged:** 2026-11-06

## Who they are, and where people meet them
Virginia's largest community college. In March 2025 it launched NOVAnext, setting aside up to $1 million in scholarships for federal employees and contractors laid off on or after 1 February 2025 who live in its service area, covering 14 courses with industry certifications plus workshops on interviewing, résumés and "using AI at work"; applications are reviewed by Virginia Career Works ([CC Daily, 13 Mar 2025](https://www.ccdaily.com/2025/03/nova-program-to-help-dislocated-federal-workers-contractors/); [NOVAnext](https://www.nvcc.edu/novanext/)). A displaced federal worker in Northern Virginia meets NOVA through this application.

## What they bring
- **Financial:** up to $1 million unrestricted funds, leveraging Virginia's FastForward short-term training program ([CC Daily](https://www.ccdaily.com/2025/03/nova-program-to-help-dislocated-federal-workers-contractors/)).
- **Reach:** NOVA hoped to serve about 1,000 people; enrollment figures not published on pages fetched ([CC Daily](https://www.ccdaily.com/2025/03/nova-program-to-help-dislocated-federal-workers-contractors/)).

## What they want
Help displaced federal workers "transition to new careers" (NOVAnext page title) in IT specialties, program management and HR ([NOVAnext](https://www.nvcc.edu/novanext/); [CC Daily](https://www.ccdaily.com/2025/03/nova-program-to-help-dislocated-federal-workers-contractors/)).

## What they've tried about this problem, and how it went
- **Fast scholarship response:** announced within six weeks of the first layoffs, with a date-based eligibility rule ([CC Daily](https://www.ccdaily.com/2025/03/nova-program-to-help-dislocated-federal-workers-contractors/)).
- **Workforce-board intake:** eligibility screening outsourced to Virginia Career Works, tying college and WIOA system together ([CC Daily](https://www.ccdaily.com/2025/03/nova-program-to-help-dislocated-federal-workers-contractors/)).
- Outcomes (enrolled, completed, employed): not found.

## How they see the problem
Mid-career workers need a quick certification and job-search coaching, paid for by scholarship (coverage). Inference: credential-conversion frame.

## Open questions for the pod
- How many NOVAnext scholarships were used of the ~1,000 target, and in which courses?
- What was in the "using AI at work" workshop, and who taught it?
