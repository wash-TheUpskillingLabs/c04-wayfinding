# Player: US Department of Labor, AI Action Plan and apprenticeship items (agency)

**Circle:** field
**Scope:** national
**Role in the problem:** policy, funder
**Logged:** 2026-11-06

## Who they are, and where people meet them
The federal workforce agency. Under the White House AI Action Plan ("Winning the Race," July 23, 2025), DOL is assigned the lead on AI workforce: AI literacy, an AI Workforce Research Hub, rapid retraining for AI-displaced workers, and expanding Registered Apprenticeships in AI-infrastructure occupations ([DOL release 25-1193-NAT](https://www.dol.gov/newsroom/releases/osec/osec20250723); [FedScoop](https://fedscoop.com/labor-department-trump-ai-action-plan-to-do-list/)). A person at The Labs meets DOL through American Job Centers, WIOA-funded providers, the apprenticeship system, and state grants such as the Industry-Driven Skills Training Fund.

## What they bring
- Financial: the Industry-Driven Skills Training Fund awarded $86M to 14 states in Dec 2025 (Oklahoma's $6M share is the documented example) ([America Achieves](https://www.americaachieves.org/articles/america-achieves-helps-oklahoma-secure-6-million-to-boost-workforce-training)). DOL's Employment and Training Administration is a partner on NSF's TechAccess: AI-Ready America ($168M–$224M; see separate file).
- Political: a stated administration goal of 1 million active apprentices, which the Labor Secretary has said she wants to exceed ([FedScoop](https://fedscoop.com/labor-department-trump-ai-action-plan-to-do-list/)).

## What they want
Per the July 2025 release: expand AI literacy nationwide, make AI skills a core goal of education and workforce funding including CTE and apprenticeships, fund rapid retraining for displaced workers, pilot pathways into entry-level roles, and define skills standards for AI-infrastructure occupations with Commerce ([DOL release](https://www.dol.gov/newsroom/releases/osec/osec20250723)). No dollar amounts or enrollment targets in the release.

## What they've tried about this problem, and how it went
- The Action Plan directs DOL to "expand the use of Registered Apprenticeships in occupations critical to AI infrastructure" and to set up the AI Workforce Research Hub ([FedScoop, Jul 25 2025](https://fedscoop.com/labor-department-trump-ai-action-plan-to-do-list/)). A Nov 24, 2025 DOL blog post claiming progress exists but redirected and could not be opened (unverified).
- Simultaneously, the administration terminated Digital Equity Act grants in May 2025 (see NDIA file) and attempted to dismantle IMLS (see IMLS file), which cut two of the channels through which community digital-skills programs had been funded.
- Outcomes of the AI-literacy and retraining items were not found published as of the sources opened.

## How they see the problem
Frame: AI as an infrastructure build-out needing skilled trades and technicians; AI literacy as a goal to be delivered through existing funded systems (CTE, apprenticeship, WIOA). Inference: community-run, non-credentialed learning is outside the frame unless it registers as pre-apprenticeship or a WIOA-eligible provider.
