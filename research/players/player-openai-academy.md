# Player: OpenAI Academy, certifications and Jobs Platform (program)

**Circle:** field
**Scope:** national
**Role in the problem:** standard-setter (credentials), competitor (for the "first step"), employer-side partner
**Logged:** 2026-11-06

## Who they are, and where people meet them
OpenAI's free education arm (Academy launched March 2025), extended in September 2025 with a plan for generative-AI certifications (pilot late 2025 or early 2026) and a jobs platform (second half of 2026), with a goal of certifying 10 million Americans by 2030; Walmart is the named launch partner, giving its 2 million associates tailored versions ([Benzinga, Sept 2025](https://benzinga.com/markets/tech/25/09/47515310/openai-to-certify-10-million-americans-in-generative-ai-by-2030-partnering-with-walmart-to-train-the-next-wave-of-ai-savvy-workers); [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/artificial-intelligence/openai-to-launch-ai-powered-jobs-platform-in-2026-certify-10-million-americans-by-2030)). A Labs participant meets it as a free self-serve course and, soon, as a credential employers may ask for.

## What they bring
- Cultural: a free, branded certificate at national scale; a jobs marketplace tied to it.
- Financial: no grant program to community organizations was found in sources opened (unverified whether one exists).

## What they want
10 million Americans certified by 2030 ([Benzinga](https://benzinga.com/markets/tech/25/09/47515310/openai-to-certify-10-million-americans-in-generative-ai-by-2030-partnering-with-walmart-to-train-the-next-wave-of-ai-savvy-workers)).

## What they've tried about this problem, and how it went
- Too new for outcomes. The design is employer-anchored (Walmart) and self-serve; whether nonprofits can deliver or proctor the certification was not stated ([Benzinga](https://benzinga.com/markets/tech/25/09/47515310/openai-to-certify-10-million-americans-in-generative-ai-by-2030-partnering-with-walmart-to-train-the-next-wave-of-ai-savvy-workers)).

## How they see the problem
Frame (inference): AI skill is tool fluency, provable by a vendor certificate and matched to jobs by the vendor's own platform.

## Open questions for the pod
- Will Greater Washington employers ask for an OpenAI certificate, and would a Labs project count alongside it?
- Can a volunteer workshop host run the certification, and on what terms?
