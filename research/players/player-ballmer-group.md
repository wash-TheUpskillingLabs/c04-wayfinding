# Player: Ballmer Group (funder)

**Circle:** field
**Scope:** national (with three regions)
**Role in the problem:** funder
**Logged:** 2026-11-06

## Who they are, and where people meet them
A family philanthropy founded 2015 whose goal is "improving economic mobility and opportunity for children and families in the United States"; it works nationally and in Southeast Michigan, Los Angeles County and Washington State ([Our Work](https://ballmergroup.org/our-work/)). Greater Washington is not one of its regions; people meet it through national grantees such as the National Fund for Workforce Solutions ([National Fund release](https://nationalfund.org/news_post/national-fund-for-workforce-solutions-awarded-grant-to-promote-economic-mobility-for-low-wage-workers), not opened) and through tools it funds for nonprofits.

## What they bring
- Financial: total giving not stated on the page opened (unverified).
- Approach: four levers: partnering with government, funding advocacy for systems change, "scaling technology and data for nonprofits," and place-based partnerships ([Our Work](https://ballmergroup.org/our-work/)).
- College & Career area funds degree, certification, training and apprenticeship pathways linked to local employers ([Our Work](https://ballmergroup.org/our-work/)).

## What they want
Economic mobility measured for children and families; large, flexible, multi-year grants (a blog post on "large-scale flexible funding" exists, [link](https://ballmergroup.org/blog/five-years-later-renewing-our-commitment-to-large-scale-flexible-funding), not opened).

## What they've tried about this problem, and how it went
- Co-funds NextLadder Ventures with Gates and others on AI for economic mobility (see Gates file; unverified details).
- Reported plan to spin regional work into independent philanthropies in its three regions ([Ballmer Group release](https://ballmergroup.org/?p=1890), search result only).

## How they see the problem
Frame: systems and data; nonprofits need shared technology. Inference: a civic platform like OLOS would be legible to it as "technology for nonprofits" if it had national reach.
