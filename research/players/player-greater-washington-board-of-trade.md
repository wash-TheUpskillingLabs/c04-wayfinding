# Player: Greater Washington Board of Trade (network)

**Circle:** field
**Scope:** regional
**Role in the problem:** policy | employer | referrer
**Logged:** 2026-11-06

## Who they are, and where people meet them
A regional business association founded in 1889 representing "hundreds of employers" across DC, Maryland and Virginia ([BOT testimony, 7 Apr 2025](https://www.bot.org/testimony-to-dc-council-workforce-strategies-must-support-economic-growth-and-resilience/)). It published a Federal Workforce Resource and Support Guide on 1 April 2025 listing job fairs and jurisdiction resources ([BOT guide](https://www.bot.org/federal-workforce-resource-support-guide-greater-washington/)). A displaced worker might meet it through that guide; a Labs organizer through its policy testimony.

## What they bring
- **Political:** testimony to the DC Council's labor committee; relationships with WTOP (partner on the guide) and COG (data source) ([BOT guide](https://www.bot.org/federal-workforce-resource-support-guide-greater-washington/)).
- **Social:** employer membership; exact count not published.

## What they want
Its April 2025 testimony asked for a regional, data-driven workforce system with shared labor-market data, employer-led work-based training with credentials recognized across the three jurisdictions, a "federal transition upskilling program," wraparound supports, and long-term outcome measurement; it said "Solving this isn't just about creating more training programs." ([BOT testimony](https://www.bot.org/testimony-to-dc-council-workforce-strategies-must-support-economic-growth-and-resilience/)).

## What they've tried about this problem, and how it went
- **Resource guide (Apr 2025, updated Oct 2025):** aggregates job fairs, state and county pages, health resources and COG data ([BOT guide](https://www.bot.org/federal-workforce-resource-support-guide-greater-washington/)). No usage data.
- Named transition fields for displaced federal workers: cybersecurity, project management, clean energy; high-growth sectors IT, clean energy, healthcare, advanced manufacturing ([BOT testimony](https://www.bot.org/testimony-to-dc-council-workforce-strategies-must-support-economic-growth-and-resilience/)).
- AI: not mentioned in the testimony (automation and digitization are).

## How they see the problem
System fragmentation across three jurisdictions and a measurement gap (short-term placements vs long-term outcomes) (testimony). Inference: an employer-demand frame that is sympathetic to cross-jurisdiction recognition.

## Open questions for the pod

- Who maintains the resource guide, and would a library cohort be listed?
