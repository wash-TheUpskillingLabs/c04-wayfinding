For the first time, The Upskilling Labs of Greater Washington has paused outside Cycles to run one on itself. Three Research Pods are rebuilding our own copy of OLOS, the open platform The Labs runs on. Scaffolding works inside each part, Front Door on where it all lives, Wayfinding on the routes between.

On Dec 8 we offer what we built to the national core. We’ve committed to at least three projects. We’re aiming for all of them.
