“Onboarding was tricky.” “Joining a project was not obvious.” “At times I did not really understand what to do next.”

Those are three people, from three different Cycles, describing the same feeling in three different ways. None of them is complaining about a workshop or a teacher. They liked those. What they’re describing is the gap in between: the moment a program ends and the next thing hasn’t shown up yet.

If you’ve ever stood in a train station watching a sign flicker while your train didn’t come, you know the feeling. Nothing is broken. You just can’t tell what to do.
