Over a few weeks we gathered {{total}} things people have said about this problem. Some came from a survey of twelve past participants. Some came from the people who run The Labs, some from the steering committee, and some from the problem owner. And some came from the 54 other programs we studied, which is why a few marks on the map are hollow.

Then we sorted every one of them by asking five plain questions: what gets in the way, who is involved, where does it happen, why does it happen, and how would each person fix it? Each question is a circle. Inside, similar statements sit together in a cluster, and the darker the ground, the more people said it.
