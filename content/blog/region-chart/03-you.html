Follow the thick blue line along the middle. You start as a newcomer, which only means you showed up. Your first stop is a session, a workshop or a training, often in a library, always free. Plenty of people come to one and that’s a good evening. If you come back, you’re a member, with an account in our app.

When you’re ready, you join a Build Cycle. That’s a sprint: you and a team take one real problem and build a first working answer to it. Halfway through, everyone votes twice, once on which problems deserve the time and once on which ideas to build. Plan on four to six hours a week.

You come out with something you built and a public record of how it got built. Some people go one step further. They teach a session, or they sit with a team that’s stuck and help it get moving. The map shows that as a line from the Cycle to the mentors and trainers, and another line from them back to the sessions.

Teaching is a fast way to find out what you really understand. We’re planning to make it a regular part of the Cycle: every participant teaches one public workshop on a skill they used.
