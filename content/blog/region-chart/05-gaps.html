Now find the red dotted lines. A map that shows only the good routes won’t help much once you start walking the real ones, so we marked the places where people leave without meaning to.

Some people come once and don’t come back. We don’t know why yet, and until we do, we can’t get better at keeping anyone.

At the vote, a project that doesn’t win takes its team with it. The people who gathered around it are suddenly without one.

When a Cycle ends, you leave with a record of what you built and no obvious next door. Employers haven’t been asked to look at it yet, and the only way back in is another Cycle.

And none of our earlier Cycles has a published write-up of what it found. Everyone who built this did it on nights and weekends, and what got built first was the thing that runs, not the account of it. That means we can’t yet show our work to the people who might back it.

Those four gaps are what this pod studies. The problem situation map shows what people told us about them.
