The outer outline is The Upskilling Labs. For now the regional association is part of it, not a separate organization: national runs the region, and a steering committee has begun the region’s self-organization toward a chartered regional organization of its own.

People

Someone hears about us and comes to a first session. If it’s worth it, they come back, become a member, and join a Cycle. They finish with a team and a record of what they built. Today, that’s where the line ends. The plan is for two lines to leave from there: one to employers who can read that record, and one back into the region as a host, trainer or contributor, who then runs the next person’s first session.

Work and code

OLOS, the Upskilling Labs app, is built and maintained by national. The region runs its own fork of it, kept current by the regional association and the steering committee, so it can change things to fit how the region works. Prototypes and merged changes from the Cycle go into the region’s fork; whatever holds up goes back to national as pull requests, so the next region starts from it. Kits and modules are planned to work the same way: written once in the commons, run in many rooms.

Knowledge

National gives the method and the Cycle’s design. The region gives the Cycle problems worth twelve weeks; the Cycle gives back finds, paradoxes and what each team learned. Sessions give members a tool or a way of working. Alumni tell us what happened next.

Money and support

Grants and partnerships move through The Upskilling Labs: funders and partners agree terms with national, not with the region. Most of what keeps the region going day to day is time and rooms: four to six volunteer hours a week from each person in a Cycle, and libraries that open their doors. The region plans to raise money of its own from sustaining regional members, for and through the Greater Washington brand. That money would still sit inside the national organization, in the region’s own sub-account. Employers in the room at the showcase are planned, not agreed.
