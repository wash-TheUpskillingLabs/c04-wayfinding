National gives every region the same platform and takes back what the regions prove. The region takes a copy it’s allowed to change. A steering committee decides what the region becomes; maintainers keep its changes compatible so they can travel back; builders turn frames into changes. That’s the top line, and its value flows in a loop: platform down, proof up.

The Cycle is a clock. It gives twelve weeks, a room on group days and a vote, and it gets problems picked, finds, prototypes and three releases. Every date on it is a place where something is due, which is what makes it a clock and not a calendar.

The person’s line is the one the other two exist for. They give four to six hours a week and what they already know. They get practice, a team and a public record of what they built. Every rail that leaves their line carries hours, a proposal or evidence upward; every rail that arrives carries a question, a frame or a team down.
