Boxes are parts of the region. Lines are what moves between them, in four kinds, and the buttons let you look at one at a time: people, work and code, knowledge, and money and support. Dashed means planned, and under each dashed line is who holds it; Unclaimed means nobody yet. Red dotted lines end in a ring where people leave. One blue line ends in an open ring: that’s what people carry out of The Labs with them.

The label on each line is our best reading. If you run the thing a line touches, you know more than the map does, and the last section shows how to fix it. On a phone, scroll sideways or open the map on its own page.
