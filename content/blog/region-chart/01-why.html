You hold a piece of the region: a pod, a project, a kit, a session, a corner of the app, a seat on the steering committee. Your piece makes sense from where you sit. From anywhere else it’s hard to see how it connects to the rest, and that goes for the people working right beside you.

So we drew all of it on one page, the parts and what passes between them. Use it to find your piece, to see what feeds it and what it feeds, and to spot the gaps nobody owns yet.

This map is part of the Wayfinding pod’s work. The pod’s question is how people find their way around The Labs, and where they get lost. Our Problem Situation Map collects the ways people told us they got lost. This one draws the ground those paths cross: the parts of the region and what moves between them.

The two fit together. Each red line here is a place where someone loses the way, and each one matches a pattern on the Problem Situation Map. Wayfinding is the work of closing those gaps, and the people who can close them are the ones reading this.
