It’s hard to hold the whole region in your head at once, so we drew it as parts and what passes between them. The boxes are the parts: national; the regional association, which for now sits inside The Upskilling Labs, and what it runs; the Cycle; partners, funders and employers; and people at each stage, from newcomers to the mentors and trainers who keep it going. The three boxes the region runs are tagged Learn, Build and Share, because that’s what each one is for. The lines are what moves. Four kinds of things move, and each has its own line, so you can follow one at a time: people, work and code, knowledge, and money and support.

Red is where people leave. Dashed is planned: not running yet. Pick one kind of line to see it on its own.
