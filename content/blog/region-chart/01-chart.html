It’s hard to hold the whole region in your head at once, so we drew it as parts and what passes between them. The boxes are the parts: national, the regional association and the two things it runs, the Cycle, partners, funders, employers, and people at each stage. The lines are what moves. Four kinds of things move, and each has its own line, so you can follow one at a time: people, work and code, knowledge, and money and support.

Red is where people leave. Dashed is planned: not running yet. Pick one kind of line to see it on its own.
