It’s hard to hold the whole region in your head at once. There’s the organisation: national, the region, a steering committee, maintainers, builders, pods, projects. There’s the Cycle, which runs on dates. There’s you, moving through both. And there’s what we’re building next. So we put all of it on one chart, a row each, and marked where the rows touch.

Read each row left to right. Its first lines say what it gives and what it gets back. When the same step appears in two rows, a connector joins them and says what passes across, and which way. Red is where people stop. Dashed is planned: not running yet.
