A map can show a library is close. It can’t make someone walk in.

The case studies keep telling us that people stay for people, and that a nudge only works with a person behind it. So the questions this prototype hands to the pod:

  • How might the map show who’ll be there, not just where?
  • How might someone who came once see the next session near them, without being chased?
  • How might a neighbor’s offer to host turn into a real session at their branch?

Like every prototype this Cycle, it goes in front of three real people next. If you try it, tell us what you looked for first.
