The Labs builds in a fixed order, and the first step is archaeology: before we decide what the problem is, we find out how it became one and what’s already been tried. So before the pod writes its paradox on Saturday, Nov 7, we read 54 programs that tried to move people from a first taste to lasting involvement, from Google Summer of Code to 4-H, and mapped 50 organizations doing related work in Greater Washington and nationally.

This post is what kept coming up. It’s a reading, not a conclusion. The paradox, and the frames that follow it, are the pod’s to write.
