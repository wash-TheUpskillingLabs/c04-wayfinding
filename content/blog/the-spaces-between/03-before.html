In both the Summer of Code data and a closer study of KDE, the students most likely to stay after the program were the ones who had already contributed before it.1 Hackathon projects lasted longer when the team’s skills were diverse and matched to the work.2 Both are settled before the event starts.

For The Labs, that points at the part we haven’t built. The Cycle is our front door today, and there’s no pre-cycle: nothing that gets someone ready, attached to a person, and doing small real work before day one.
