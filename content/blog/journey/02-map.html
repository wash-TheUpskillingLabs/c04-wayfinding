Read it like the Metro. The blue line is the journey, left to right, and dashed means planned and not running yet. Red rings are where people leave, the arcs above are people skipping ahead or coming back, and the outlined columns are the Cycle.

Inside the Cycle, the line works like track that several lines share. When proposals are open, they run side by side as a bundle: problems, then paradoxes, then frames, then projects. At a vote, some proposals end at a terminus bar and the chosen ones branch off. Vote 1 sends pods off on their own lines, and Vote 2 sends projects off inside each pod. Every project comes back together at the showcase. How many pods and projects form is decided in the Cycle, not in advance, so the map draws a few and the dots say there may be more, or fewer.

The bottom row quotes the pod’s statements: A is the alumni survey of the first three Cycles, O is organizers. Where it says nothing heard yet, we haven’t asked anyone who was there.
