The blue line is the journey, left to right. Dashed means planned and not running yet. Red rings are where people leave. The arcs above the line are people skipping ahead or coming back. The shaded columns are the Cycle.

The strip across the top shows the shape of the work. Four times in a Cycle, people make proposals and the options open up: problems, then paradoxes, then frames, then projects. Each time, a choice narrows them, and the Cycle branches. Vote 1 picks problems and a pod forms around each one, so one path becomes several. Inside each pod, paradox and frame proposals narrow to that pod’s own paradox and frames. Vote 2 picks projects, so each pod splits again, and every project builds toward the same showcase. Before the Cycle, the dashed funnel is the many ways people find us, all ending at one door. After it, the work fans out into wherever people take it.

The bottom row quotes the pod’s statements: A is the alumni survey of the first three Cycles, O is organizers. Where it says nothing heard yet, we haven’t asked anyone who was there.
