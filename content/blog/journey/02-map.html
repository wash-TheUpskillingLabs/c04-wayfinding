The thick blue line is your path, left to right. Dashed means planned and not running yet. The thin lines around it are everyone else’s. Red rings are where people leave, the arcs above are people skipping ahead or coming back, and the shaded columns are the Cycle.

Watch the shape the path runs through. Before the Cycle, many ways in run into one line. Inside it, proposals open up four times and a choice narrows them each time: problems, then paradoxes, then frames, then projects. Vote 1 picks problems and a pod forms around each one, so one path becomes several. Inside each pod, paradox and frame proposals narrow to that pod’s own paradox and frames. Vote 2 picks projects, so each pod splits again, and every project builds toward the same showcase. After it, the line fans out into wherever people take it.

The bottom row quotes the pod’s statements: A is the alumni survey of the first three Cycles, O is organizers. Where it says nothing heard yet, we haven’t asked anyone who was there.
