Before we built anything, we read 54 programs that tried to move people from a first taste to something lasting. The same lessons keep showing up.

  1. The cliff is the default. Every program loses most of its people the moment it ends.
  2. Saying isn’t staying. Most people say they’ll keep going. It predicts almost nothing.
  3. The real work happens before day one. Who stays is mostly settled before the program starts.
  4. A message is only as good as the person behind it. Reminders work when someone real stands behind them.
  5. People stay for people. The project ends. The people don’t.
  6. Heroes are a single point of failure. Build for the neighborhoods, not one devoted person.
