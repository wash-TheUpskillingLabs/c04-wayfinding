      About the pod

      Wayfinding is one of three Research Pods in Build Cycle C04. We study how people find their way between the parts of The Labs, and build what helps them choose the route that fits.

      What we believe

      Anyone should be able to see where they are, choose where to go next, and find the people going there with them.

      When that’s true, people stop waiting to be told what’s next. They form teams, learn by building, and carry what they learned somewhere new. That’s the agency The Labs exists to build. Right now, the routes between our own parts get in its way.

      Where people get lost at The Labs

      The Labs started in DC Public Library branches in 2025. In under a year, on volunteer hours alone, about a thousand people have come through.

      What gets in their way is rarely the room. It’s the spaces between: how to start, what to do this week, how to join a team, where to go when the Cycle ends. And it isn’t just us.

      Here, we asked twelve past participants for the one thing they’d change. Nine named a moment between the parts.

      Asked what mattered most, almost all of them named the people they worked with.

      Twelve isn’t many, and they’re the ones who stayed. People who got lost at the door didn’t answer. They’re who we want to hear from next.

      How we’re working

      We study the problem before we solve it, with frame creation, a method from the designer Kees Dorst. All of it happens in public.

      1. Read what’s been tried. 54 programs and 50 organizations so far.
      2. Hear from people who lived it. Especially the ones who left.
      3. Name the paradox. Nov 7.
      4. Try new frames. Quick prototypes, three real people each.
      5. Build what holds up. Shipped by Dec 8.

      What we’re building

      For the first time, The Upskilling Labs of Greater Washington has paused outside Cycles to run one on itself. Three Research Pods are rebuilding our own copy of OLOS, the open platform The Labs runs on. Scaffolding works inside each part, Front Door on where it all lives, Wayfinding on the routes between.

      On Dec 8 we offer what we built to the national core. We’ve committed to at least three projects. We’re aiming for all of them.
